# Gaming Catalog Skill

Read `../../knowledge/LAMMAH-DESIGN-BIBLE.md`.

## Identity

Players remember gameplay: maps, levels, objectives, weapons, items, abilities,
characters, vehicles, interfaces, sounds, strategies, boss fights, and match
moments.

## Recommended Question Patterns

Map or Location Recognition, Object Recognition, Sound Recognition, Visual
Detail, Action Recognition, Technique or Ability Recognition, Plan Recognition,
Battle or Match Event, Outcome Recognition, and limited Character
Identification.

## Media and Difficulty

Prefer real gameplay screenshots and bounded gameplay clips, then distinctive
sound effects or music. Text is appropriate for meaningful mechanics or lore.
Do not force audio or video where the game lacks a strong Asset. Difficulty
comes from prominence, attention, mechanical depth, and fair scene recall.

Reject release dates, developers, publishers, sales, awards, production facts,
selection-screen or promotional identity giveaways, Direct Relationship, and
decorative Media unless the Subject explicitly targets industry knowledge.
