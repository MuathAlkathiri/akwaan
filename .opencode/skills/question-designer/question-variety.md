# Question Pattern Variety

A Generated Batch must vary underlying challenges, not merely wording.

## Planning Rules

- Resolve suitable Question Patterns from Subject, then Catalog, then generic
  support.
- Do not force every Catalog to use every pattern.
- Avoid accidental concentration in one pattern, answer, event, scene, Asset,
  required observation, or recognition channel.
- Direct Character Identification may not exceed 15% of the batch:
  `floor(count * 0.15)`.
- Direct Relationship is not a default Question Pattern.
- A fixed per-template ceiling does not scale; use a batch plan appropriate to
  the requested count.

Repeating a prompt such as "Who is this character?" is allowed only when answers
and Assets differ, the direct-identification cap passes, and the batch remains
meaningfully varied.

## Review

Compare planned and final Pattern, answer, event, and Media distributions.
Replace candidates that make the batch repetitive even when their wording is
different.
