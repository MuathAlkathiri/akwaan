# Pattern: Two Groups

- Experience goal: rapid binary distinction with discussion.
- Interaction shape: place six to ten items into two labeled groups.
- ContentItem shape: set, two labels, and canonical mapping.
- Answer payload: `{ "groups": [...], "itemToGroup": {...} }`.
- Machine resolution: exact mapping comparison.
- Constraints: both groups contain at least two items; membership is exclusive.
- Compatibility: any Scope with stable binary classifications.
- Media: optional text or comparable assets.
- Tension levers: familiar near-neighbors and mixed presentation order.
- Anti-patterns: overlapping groups, subjective labels, identity stereotypes.
- Valid example: football clubs by English or Spanish league.
- Invalid example: characters divided into good or bad when alignment changes.
