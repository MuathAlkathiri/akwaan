# Pattern: Three Groups

- Experience goal: layered classification with coordination.
- Interaction shape: place six to nine items into three labeled groups.
- ContentItem shape: set, three labels, and canonical mapping.
- Answer payload: `{ "groups": [...], "itemToGroup": {...} }`.
- Machine resolution: exact mapping comparison.
- Constraints: each group has at least two items; no dual membership.
- Compatibility: Scopes with three stable, audience-readable classes.
- Media: optional and consistent across the set.
- Tension levers: visual or semantic similarity across classes.
- Anti-patterns: tiny classes, disputed taxonomy, hidden exceptions.
- Valid example: anime entities by three clearly named factions at one time.
- Invalid example: works assigned to loosely defined mood labels.
