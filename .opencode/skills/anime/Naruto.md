# Naruto Skill

## Purpose

This skill extends the Anime Question Design Skill.

It defines what Naruto fans remember and what should be prioritized when creating Naruto questions.

---

# Naruto Identity

Naruto is heavily driven by memorable characters, emotional scenes and iconic abilities.

Most enjoyable questions come from recognition rather than factual recall.

# Easy Difficulty

Prioritize:

- Main characters as event, action, quote, or technique context—not as Direct
  Character Identification answers
- Akatsuki
- Sharingan
- Rasengan
- Chidori
- Kurama
- Hokage
- Famous fights

---

# Medium Difficulty

Prioritize:

- Sage Mode scenes
- Mangekyo abilities
- Supporting characters
- Summons
- Famous quotes
- Village symbols

---

# Hard Difficulty

Prioritize:

- Brief but memorable scenes
- Lesser-used jutsu
- Secondary characters
- Short appearances
- Advanced lore that dedicated fans commonly remember

Hard should still reward recognition.

Never rely on obscure facts.

---

# Things To Avoid

Avoid questions focused on:

- Filler episodes
- Episode numbers
- Production trivia
- Animation staff
- Voice actors
- Manga chapter numbers
- Extremely obscure lore

---

# Direct Identification Exclusions

The following are obvious and must not be answers to Direct Character
Identification:

- Naruto
- Sasuke
- Sakura
- Kakashi
- Itachi

This list is inferred from the central cast and prominence already documented
above. These characters remain valid in event, action, quote, technique,
sequence, and cause-and-effect questions.

# Preferred Question Patterns

Prioritize Scene Recognition, Event Recall, What Happens Next, Battle Event,
Technique or Ability Recognition, Transformation Recognition, Quote Attribution,
Motivation, Object Recognition, and Location Recognition. Use Direct Character
Identification sparingly and only for non-obvious meaningful characters.

# Restricted Patterns

Reject Direct Relationship, production trivia, filler-dependent trivia,
decorative Media, and promotional art that makes an identity automatic.
