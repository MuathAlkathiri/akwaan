# Football Catalog Skill

Read `../../knowledge/LAMMAH-DESIGN-BIBLE.md`.

## Identity

Football creates gameplay through players, clubs, kits, stadiums, goals,
assists, saves, celebrations, tactics, match sequences, tournaments, and
meaningful records.

## Recommended Question Patterns

Battle or Match Event, What Happens Next, Who Was Present, Sequence Recognition,
Plan Recognition, Visual Detail, Action and Outcome Recognition, kit or location
recognition, sound recognition when distinctive, and meaningful Knowledge
Questions.

## Media and Difficulty

Prefer match frames and bounded clips whose answer requires observation. Use
audio only for distinctive commentary, chants, or sounds with verifiable rights
and attribution. Difficulty comes from event prominence, player prominence,
competition context, and sequence depth—not bad crops or obscure statistics.

Avoid generic headshot identification, promotional renders, repeated player
answers, Direct Relationship, decorative Media, and insignificant statistics.
Current facts require reliable current research; historical facts require
authoritative support.
