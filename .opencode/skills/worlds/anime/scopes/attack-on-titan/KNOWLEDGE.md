# Attack on Titan Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Durable sets include military branches, walls, districts, equipment, Titan
forms, factions, locations, operations, political institutions, and major events.

## Stable Relationships and Classifications

Military branch, home district, equipment type, named operation, faction, and
revealed Titan class can support deterministic grouping when tied to an era.

## Media Anchors

Uniform insignia, maneuver gear, walls and districts, Titan silhouettes,
weapons, locations, voices, and bounded action frames.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Identity reveals, shifting factions, translated military titles, motives, and
late-series geography are high-risk. Use official anime episodes and licensed
reference material, recording the precise spoiler endpoint.
