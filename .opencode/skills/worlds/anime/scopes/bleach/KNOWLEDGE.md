# Bleach Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Core vocabulary includes Soul Reapers (شينيغامي), Hollows, Arrancars, Quincies,
Visored, Gotei 13 captains and lieutenants, Zanpakutō and their Bankai, Seireitei,
Karakura Town, Hueco Mundo, Las Noches, Espada ranks, and named arcs. Use
established Arabic fandom spellings with verified romanized aliases.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Gotei 13 divisions, captains, lieutenants, and seated officers within the spoiler boundary.
- Espada and notable Arrancars, Hollows, Quincies, and Visored.
- Seireitei, Karakura Town, Hueco Mundo, Las Noches, and other named locations.
- Zanpakutō names, abilities, weapons, artifacts, and major events.

## Stable Classifications

Division assignment, captain-to-lieutenant pairing, organization membership,
race or kind, rank (including Espada numbers), and arc-era presence are usable
when the selected era makes them deterministic.

## Numeric Dimensions

Division numbers, Espada ranks, membership counts, ages, and episode numbers
require an explicit canonical snapshot because many change over time. Never use
a live or unlabeled value.

## Media Anchors

Division insignia, captain haori, Shinigami uniforms, Hollow masks, Zanpakutō
forms, Espada number tattoos, distinctive environments, voices, and bounded
event frames. Avoid frames whose subtitles or HUD labels expose the result.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Translated titles, transliteration variants, filler continuity, temporary
alliances, identity reveals, and Bankai reveals are high risk. Prefer official
anime episodes and licensed reference material; record the spoiler endpoint and
source date with every unstable claim. Translated-title and Bankai-reveal
spoilers must never leak through media, prompts, or metadata.
