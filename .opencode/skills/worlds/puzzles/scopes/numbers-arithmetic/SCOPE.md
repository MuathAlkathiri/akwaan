# Scope: Numbers and Arithmetic

- `scopeId`: `puzzles.numbers-arithmetic`
- `worldId`: `puzzles`
- Boundary: everyday arithmetic and number play: addition, subtraction,
  multiplication, division, sequences, digit puzzles, and simple fractions
  presented as puzzle material.
- Included: arithmetic expressions, missing operators, number sequences, digit
  arrangements, magnitudes, dates, prices, ages, and simple counting.
- Excluded: advanced mathematics, algebra that needs a formula sheet,
  ambiguous digit tricks, and any computation the team cannot do in their head
  in seconds.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Pattern exclusions: recall of memorized tables only; every item must be
  computable from the instruction and fragments alone.
- Safety: no real personal data, no live prices, and no values that change.
