# Numbers and Arithmetic

## Identity and Vocabulary

Puzzle material is arithmetic you can do in your head: adding, subtracting,
multiplying, dividing, sequences, digit puzzles, and simple fractions. Use
Western digits with standard Arabic phrasing.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Small integer sums, differences, products, and quotients.
- Simple number sequences with one clear rule.
- Digit arrangements and place-value tricks.
- Halves, quarters, and simple fractions with whole-number results.
- Everyday counts: days, hours, sides, wheels, and similar fixed counts.

## Stable Relationships and Classifications

Operator-to-result, term-to-next, and digit-to-place links are stable when the
rule is stated or deducible from the fragments.

## Numeric Dimensions

Numbers are the core: every digit, sign, and order shown in a fragment must be
exact and unchangeable.

## Media Anchors

Number tiles, equation strips, sequence cards, and grids. Remove any symbol or
label that reveals the result.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Order of operations, negative results, and leading zeros are common risks. State
the operation order in the instruction or design the fragments so only one order
works.
