# Scope: Shapes and Patterns

- `scopeId`: `puzzles.shapes-patterns`
- `worldId`: `puzzles`
- Boundary: visual puzzles: shapes, patterns, sequences of figures, matrices,
  symmetry, and simple spatial reasoning presented as puzzle material.
- Included: shape sequences, pattern repeats, matrices, symmetry, rotation,
  reflection, counting shapes, and figure completion.
- Excluded: puzzles that need precise measurement, animated or time-based
  figures, and any layout a phone screen cannot render clearly.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Pattern exclusions: description-only items; shapes must be describable aloud
  in Arabic without seeing them.
- Safety: no flashing figures and no layouts that strain the eyes.
