# Scope: Logic and Deduction

- `scopeId`: `puzzles.logic-deduction`
- `worldId`: `puzzles`
- Boundary: classic deduction puzzles: true/false statements, clues, ordering,
  matching, and simple syllogisms presented as puzzle material.
- Included: yes/no clues, ordering rules, matching problems, true/false
  statements, simple logic grids, and constraints the team can reason through
  aloud.
- Excluded: formal logic notation, puzzle conditions with too many variables
  for two-minute play, and any step that requires pen and paper.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Pattern exclusions: guessing games; every item must have a deterministic
  deduction path.
- Safety: no real people or groups; use neutral objects, colors, and everyday
  things.
