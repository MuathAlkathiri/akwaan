# Scope: General Knowledge

- `scopeId`: `puzzles.general-knowledge`
- `worldId`: `puzzles`
- Boundary: everyday shared knowledge that most players recognize without
  looking it up: geography, science basics, history highlights, landmarks,
  food, sports, cultures, and common symbols.
- Included: stable everyday facts, famous landmarks, common geography,
  everyday science, well-known historical markers, foods, sports, and shared
  cultural basics.
- Excluded: obscure or disputed facts, specialist trivia, date-sensitive or
  live facts, region-specific facts a Saudi/MENA team cannot be expected to
  know, and anything the host must verify mid-game.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Pattern exclusions: recall-only items; every puzzle must stay solvable from
  the instruction and fragments without outside knowledge.
- Safety: no politics, religion-specific claims, personal data, or
  age-restricted material.
