# Scope: Letters and Words

- `scopeId`: `puzzles.letters-words`
- `worldId`: `puzzles`
- Boundary: the Arabic alphabet and everyday Arabic words: letter sets,
  syllables, common word parts, and simple word play that a phone screen can
  show.
- Included: letter tiles, mixed letters, missing letters, syllables, short
  everyday words, opposites, synonyms, compound words, and simple word
  relationships in Arabic.
- Excluded: rare letters and diacritics that render unreliably, words with
  disputed spellings, offensive words, and any puzzle that depends on fonts the
  phone may not have.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Pattern exclusions: nothing that requires a dictionary or lookup; every item
  must resolve from the instruction and fragments alone.
- Safety: no real-person names, brands, or sensitive words; keep to everyday
  neutral vocabulary.
