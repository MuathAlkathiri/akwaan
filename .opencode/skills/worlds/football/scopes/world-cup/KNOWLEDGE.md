# World Cup Knowledge

Durable sets include editions, host nations, participating national teams,
champions, finalists, venues, iconic matches, notable goals, captains, awards,
official balls, mascots, and tournament stages.

Useful classifications include confederation, tournament stage, champion status,
host region, playing position, and named edition. Numeric values must identify
the edition and official source. Media anchors include kits, stadiums, trophy,
scoreboards with result-safe crops, commentary, and recognizable match moments.

Historical rules, team names, formats, and record definitions change. Prefer
FIFA sources and authoritative match records; label the exact edition.
