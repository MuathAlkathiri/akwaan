# Movies Catalog Skill

Read `../../knowledge/LAMMAH-DESIGN-BIBLE.md`.

## Identity

Movie audiences remember scenes, characters, dialogue, objects, locations,
decisions, reveals, confrontations, and outcomes. Prefer story experience over
production facts.

## Recommended Question Patterns

Scene Recognition, Event Recall, What Happens Next, Dialogue Completion, Quote
Attribution, Motivation, Cause and Effect, Object and Location Recognition,
Visual Detail, Action Recognition, and limited Character Identification.

## Media and Difficulty

Prefer in-film frames and bounded clips; use audio for distinctive dialogue,
music, or sound; use text for meaningful plot knowledge. Easy uses famous but
non-trivial moments, Medium rewards regular viewers, and Hard uses less
prominent yet memorable story details.

Reject actors, directors, writers, studios, dates, awards, budgets, promotional
artwork, Direct Relationship questions, decorative Media, and obvious-character
Direct Identification unless the requested Subject explicitly targets
production trivia.
