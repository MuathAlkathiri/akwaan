# Content Coverage Ledger

Maintain a ledger before drafting and update it after every approval, rejection,
or replacement.

For each candidate record:

- World;
- Scope;
- compatible Challenge Type(s);
- answer mode;
- Content Pattern;
- Gameplay Pattern;
- primary focus;
- context entities;
- event cluster;
- answer;
- media type;
- source/evidence;
- leakage result;
- asset blind-test result;
- duplicate status;
- readiness status.

The ledger replaces legacy difficulty/points allocation. Its purpose is to prevent
repetitive sets and expose coverage gaps before final output.
