# Accessibility and Pacing

Akwaan does not store Easy/Medium/Hard and does not use 200/400/600 tiers.

Evaluate each ContentItem using:

- target-audience familiarity;
- recognition saturation;
- number of reasoning steps;
- visual/audio clarity;
- ambiguity;
- wording load;
- time needed under the mechanic's fixed timer;
- reveal value.

Prefer familiar-but-not-automatic material. Avoid both trivial instant answers
and obscure facts no player would enjoy revealing.

Never manufacture challenge through blur, tiny crops, bad audio, trick wording,
missing context, production trivia, or multiple valid answers.
