# Face Fusion Rules

## Candidate Selection

Choose people that are:

- famous
- visually distinctive
- recognizable to fans

Prefer:

- iconic hairstyles
- facial hair
- eye shape
- smiles
- expressions

Avoid:

- similar-looking people
- blurry references
- side profiles
- helmets
- sunglasses
- hidden faces

---

## Fusion Quality

Both identities must survive.

The merged face should immediately make the player think:

"I know this person... wait... that's two people."

Not:

"I have no idea what I'm looking at."

---

## Accessibility calibration

Easy

Very famous pair.

Example:

Messi + Cristiano

Medium

Well-known players.

Hard

Recognizable but less obvious combinations.

Challenge comes from the pairing.

Not from bad image quality.
