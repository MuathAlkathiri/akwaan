# Object Questions

## Purpose

Design trivia questions about recognizable objects, weapons, tools, documents, products, or symbolic items from a television series.

## Preferred Question Styles

- ما اسم هذا الشيء؟
- لمن يعود هذا الغرض؟
- ما استخدام هذا الشيء؟
- ما اسم هذا السلاح؟
- أي شخصية استخدمت هذا الغرض؟
- ماذا يحتوي هذا الشيء؟
- ما أهمية هذا الغرض في القصة؟

## Suitable Objects

- Weapons
- Tools
- Documents
- Letters
- Phones
- Vehicles keys
- Costumes
- Masks
- Jewelry
- Containers
- Products
- Scientific equipment
- Symbolic objects
- Personal belongings
- Evidence used in an investigation

## Accessibility calibration

### Easy

Use iconic and visually recognizable objects.

The object should be strongly associated with a main character or famous scene.

### Medium

Use objects connected to important events or supporting characters.

The player may need to remember the object's owner, purpose, or story relevance.

### Hard

Use less prominent objects or ask about specific details.

Examples:

- Who owned the object first?
- What was hidden inside it?
- In which event was it used?
- What consequence resulted from using it?

## Media Rules

The selected image must:

- Show the object clearly.
- Keep the important visual details visible.
- Avoid excessive background distractions.
- Avoid showing the answer in labels, subtitles, or captions.
- Avoid generic objects unless their appearance is unique in the series.

## Validation Rules

Reject the question if:

- The object is visually generic and cannot be uniquely identified.
- Multiple characters own identical versions of the object.
- The expected name is not used officially in the series.
- The object exists only in external promotional material.
- The answer depends on fan terminology.
- The media does not clearly show the object.
- More than one answer could reasonably be accepted.

Prefer objects that are memorable, distinctive, and relevant to the story.
