# Geography Catalog Skill

Read `../../knowledge/AKWAN-CONTENT-BIBLE.md`.

## Identity

Geography balances meaningful Knowledge Questions with recognition of maps,
landforms, skylines, flags, borders, and culturally significant locations.

## allowedQuestionPatterns

- `WHY_DID`
- `WHAT_HAPPENED_NEXT`
- `WHAT_HAPPENED_BEFORE`
- `WHAT_WAS_THE_GOAL`
- `SCENE_RECOGNITION`
- `OBJECT_RECOGNITION`
- `LOCATION_RECOGNITION`
- `ORGANIZATION`
- `EVENT`
- `SEQUENCE`
- `TIMELINE`
- `KNOWLEDGE`

## Pattern Priorities

Location Recognition, Map Recognition, Visual Detail, Cause and Effect,
Sequence Recognition where meaningful, and factual Knowledge Questions.

## Media and accessibility calibration

Use images or maps only when they are essential and answer-safe. Do not force
audio or video. Challenge comes from location prominence, fair visual context,
and meaningful geographic relationships—not unlabeled ambiguity, tiny map
details, or arbitrary statistics.

Prefer authoritative geographic sources and verify time-sensitive political or
population facts. Reject decorative Media and questions with multiple reasonable
locations.
