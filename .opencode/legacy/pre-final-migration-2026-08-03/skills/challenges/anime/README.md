# Anime Challenge Skill Pack

This pack is governed by `.opencode/knowledge/AKWAN-CONTENT-BIBLE.md` and
`.opencode/skills/worlds/anime/SKILL.md`. Challenges are gameplay lenses applied
to selected Anime Content Scopes; they are not Scope folders or media buckets.
Every concept belongs to exactly one Challenge. Use
`CHALLENGE-BOUNDARIES.md` when signals overlap.

| Challenge | One-line identity | Primary gameplay signal | Common media | Nearest overlap risk | Skill |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Otaku | Meaningful anime knowledge, context, and deduction | Recall or reasoning about lore, relationships, history, and rules | Text, structured comparisons, occasional evidentiary media | Absorbing recognition tasks as general trivia | `otaku/SKILL.md` |
| Characters | Recognition of a character through identity cues | Appearance, silhouette, mannerism, or constrained identity evidence | Stills, crops, silhouettes, short loops | Biography routed as identity, or identity routed by voice | `characters/SKILL.md` |
| Abilities | Recognition or understanding of powers and systems | Technique effect, activation, rule, limit, or transformation mechanic | Focused clips, frames, audio effects, diagrams | User identity, battle event, or weapon identity doing the work | `abilities/SKILL.md` |
| Voices | Recognition of nonmusical vocal performance | Timbre, laugh, delivery, cadence, or spoken performance | Clean short audio, visually neutral clips | Dialogue knowledge, opening music, or scene context | `voices/SKILL.md` |
| Scenes | Recognition or understanding of a specific event | Depicted action, turning point, immediate cause, or consequence | Stills, short clips, ordered frames | Simple character/place/ability recognition | `scenes/SKILL.md` |
| Openings | Recognition and association of anime opening themes or sequences | Opening music, choreography, motif, sequence, or era | Audio excerpts, opening footage, motif frames | Voice, episode scene, or production trivia | `openings/SKILL.md` |
| Places | Recognition or spatial understanding of locations | Architecture, geography, atmosphere, layout, or landmark | Establishing shots, panoramas, maps, spatial diagrams | Foreground character/event or detached symbol supplying the clue | `places/SKILL.md` |
| Symbols and Objects | Recognition and association of designed things | Object form, markings, construction, use, or symbolic identity | Close-ups, isolated models, silhouettes, use clips | Character owner, ability effect, or lore history doing the work | `symbols-and-objects/SKILL.md` |

## Use Order

1. Read the Product Bible.
2. Read the Anime World Skill.
3. Select one Challenge by its primary solving mechanic and source of fun.
4. Read that Challenge Skill.
5. Select the permitted Content Scope or Scopes and read their knowledge.
6. Design, review, curate, and validate only within that ownership decision.

Variety is managed inside the selected Challenge through subjects, actions,
formats, eras, tones, and Scopes. It never creates a separate ownership layer.
