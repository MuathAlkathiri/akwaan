# VOICE_RECOGNITION

## Purpose

Recognize a character, commentator, source, or moment from essential audio.
Required Media: a clean bounded audio/video clip.

Do not use generic dialogue, introductions naming the answer, music-title trivia
unless the Subject allows it, or clips obscured by narration/noise.

Validation: voice is distinctive, text gives no independent identity clue, and
aliases are supported. Arabic example: `من الشخصية التي تتحدث في المقطع؟`
