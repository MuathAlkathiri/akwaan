# KNOWLEDGE

## Purpose

Test meaningful, fan-relevant knowledge that is worth a batch slot. Media is
normally absent unless essential to the fact being tested.

Do not use production trivia, arbitrary dates/numbers, obscure facts, or a
definition that reconstructs the answer.

Validation: factual support, one bounded answer, Familiar But Not Immediate at
broad-audience accessibility, and discussion/reveal value. Arabic example:
`من هو الهوكاجي الثالث في قرية الورق؟`
