# WHAT_WAS_THE_GOAL

## Purpose

Test the explicit objective of a plan, mission, strategy, or action. Media is
optional unless it identifies the plan being observed.

Do not confuse intended goal with actual outcome or speculative motive.
Validation requires a canonically stated, concise objective.

Arabic example: `ما كان هدف الخطة التي بدأت في هذا المشهد؟`
