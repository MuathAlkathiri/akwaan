# WHAT_HAPPENED_BEFORE

## Purpose

Test the event immediately preceding a clear scene or outcome. Image/video may
anchor the endpoint; text may anchor a famous consequence.

Do not use when the preceding boundary is editorially ambiguous or several
parallel actions qualify.

Validation: require the nearest meaningful cause/beat. Arabic example:
`ماذا حدث مباشرة قبل ظهور هذه النتيجة؟`
