# ORGANIZATION / TEAM

## Purpose

Recognize or recall a meaningful organization, house, faction, club, or team
through emblem, action, membership at a stated time, or event role.
Media is optional; emblems, uniforms, flags, or group scenes are suitable only
when they are essential and do not display the answer.

Do not show the complete name, ask an obvious relationship, or omit timeframe
when membership/leadership changes.

Validation: canonical group and one bounded answer. Arabic example:
`ما اسم المنظمة التي تستخدم هذا الرمز؟`
