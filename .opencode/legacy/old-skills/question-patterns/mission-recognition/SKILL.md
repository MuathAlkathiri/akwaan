# MISSION_RECOGNITION

Recognize a mission from a distinctive playable location, action, objective, or
bounded scene. Verify title and mission name together. Reject mission-title UI,
generic combat, production trivia, wrong-title attribution, and cinematic
footage that does not provide fair mission-specific evidence.

Arabic example: `من هذه اللقطة، ما اسم المهمة؟`
