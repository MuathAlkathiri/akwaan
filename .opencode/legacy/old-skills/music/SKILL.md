# Music Catalog Skill

Read `../../knowledge/LAMMAH-DESIGN-BIBLE.md`.

## Identity

Music audiences remember melodies, intros, voices, lyrics, instruments,
performances, rhythm, and culturally memorable songs.

## allowedQuestionPatterns

- `COMPLETE_QUOTE`
- `DIALOGUE`
- `VOICE_RECOGNITION`
- `SCENE_RECOGNITION`
- `OBJECT_RECOGNITION`
- `LOCATION_RECOGNITION`
- `ORGANIZATION`
- `TEAM`
- `EVENT`
- `SEQUENCE`
- `TIMELINE`
- `KNOWLEDGE`

## Pattern Priorities

Sound Recognition, Voice Recognition, Dialogue or Lyric Completion where
legally usable, Quote Attribution, Visual Detail, Performance Recognition, and
meaningful Knowledge Questions about the music itself.

## Media and Difficulty

Audio is preferred only when a short, lawful, local clip is available and
essential. Images and video should test performance or visual details rather
than decorate artist facts. Difficulty comes from track or performance
prominence and the amount of fair audio context.

Reject production-company trivia, release-date memorization, awards by default,
generic artist headshots, decorative Media, answer-bearing titles/metadata, and
obscure clips without a fair recognition hook. Respect the project's existing
copyright and attribution handling.
