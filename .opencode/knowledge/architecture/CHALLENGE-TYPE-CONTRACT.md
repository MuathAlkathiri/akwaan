# ChallengeType Contract

Every ChallengeType skill defines: Experience Goal, Social Dynamic, Player
Emotion, Interaction Pattern, Thinking Pattern, Success Pattern, Failure
Pattern, Input Contract, Resolution Contract, Content Structure, Allowed
Content Patterns, Content Safety Rules, Media Compatibility, Scope
Compatibility, Validation Rules, and Anti-patterns.

ChallengeTypes are global. World-specific naming and presentation are
configuration, not separate mechanics.
