# WHO_WAS_WITH

## Purpose

Test presence in a specific scene, trip, meeting, or action—not a permanent
relationship. Image/video is preferred when presence must be observed.

Do not use as `من هو صديقه؟` or when several unseen participants may
qualify. Group answers must be closed and counted.

Validation: specify the bounded moment and required number. Arabic example:
`من كان معه عندما دخل القصر؟`
