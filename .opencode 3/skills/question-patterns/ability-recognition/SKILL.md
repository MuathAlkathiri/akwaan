# ABILITY_RECOGNITION

## Purpose

Recognize a technique, power, mechanic, or transformation from its actual use or
effect in a memorable moment. Image, video, or distinctive sound is preferred.

Do not define or translate the ability name in the wording, attach decorative
Media, or use visually indistinguishable abilities.

Validation: one canonical ability and no semantic answer leakage. Arabic
example: `ما اسم التقنية المستخدمة في هذه اللحظة؟`
