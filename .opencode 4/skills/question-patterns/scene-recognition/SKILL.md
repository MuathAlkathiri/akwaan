# SCENE_RECOGNITION

## Purpose

Recognize an event, stage, arc, or meaningful detail from an in-world scene.
Required Media: image or bounded video that is essential.

Do not use promotional art, decorative frames, generic locations, or a scene
whose subtitles reveal the answer.

Validation: one intended scene/event and fair visible context. Arabic example:
`ما الحدث الذي يظهر في هذا المشهد؟`
