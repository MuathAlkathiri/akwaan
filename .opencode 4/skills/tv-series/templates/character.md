# Character Questions

Preferred styles:

- من هذه الشخصية؟
- ما اسم هذه الشخصية؟
- لأي مسلسل تنتمي هذه الشخصية؟

Images should:

- Clearly show the character.
- Avoid costumes that hide identity.
- Avoid multiple main characters.
- Come from a natural in-story frame rather than a poster, promotional render,
  or clean transparent portrait.

Direct Character Identification may not exceed 15% of the Generated Batch.
Never use the central, title, mascot, or top-five obvious character as its
answer.

Difficulty

Easy

Recognizable non-obvious supporting characters in clear in-story framing.

Medium

Supporting characters.

Hard

Minor characters.
