# SOUND_RECOGNITION

Recognize a game, mechanic, item, event, state, or cue from clean bounded audio.
Use original/extracted game audio or a faithful clean capture. The sound must
support one intended answer without generic gunfire, noise, narration, or the
full answer being spoken. Record the required audible observation and reject
audio that is distinctive only after the text supplies the answer.

Arabic example: `هذا الصوت يدل على أي حدث في اللعبة؟`
