# Relationship Reveal Questions

Direct Relationship questions are deprecated and rejected by default.

Allowed Question Patterns use a relationship only within a memorable discovery,
betrayal, mystery, reveal, or event:

- من اكتشف حقيقة العلاقة؟
- ما الحدث الذي كشف صلة الشخصيتين؟
- من بقي غير مدرك للحقيقة؟

The relationship must be officially confirmed in the television canon and have
one answer. Prefer Event Recall or Cause and Effect. Reject obvious questions
such as "Who is X's father/wife/friend?"

Never use fan theories.
