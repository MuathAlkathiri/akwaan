# Organization Questions

## Purpose

Design trivia questions about organizations, families, houses, companies, gangs, institutions, teams, and political groups within a television series.

## Preferred Question Styles

- ما اسم هذه المنظمة؟
- إلى أي عائلة تنتمي هذه الشخصية؟
- لأي منزل ينتمي هذا الشعار؟
- ما اسم هذه الشركة؟
- ما اسم هذه العصابة؟
- من يقود هذه المنظمة؟
- ما الهدف الرئيسي لهذه الجماعة؟
- أي شخصية تعمل لصالح هذه الجهة؟

## Supported Organization Types

- Families
- Houses
- Companies
- Government agencies
- Criminal organizations
- Gangs
- Political groups
- Military groups
- Religious groups
- Secret organizations
- Schools
- Laboratories
- Law-enforcement agencies
- Business groups

## Difficulty

### Easy

Use famous organizations strongly associated with the series.

Examples:

- Main families
- Iconic companies
- Major government agencies
- Famous criminal groups

### Medium

Use secondary organizations or ask about leadership and membership.

Examples:

- Who leads the organization?
- Which character works there?
- What is its main activity?
- Where is its headquarters?

### Hard

Use internal divisions, changing leadership, alliances, rivals, or historical details.

Examples:

- Which organization replaced another?
- Who secretly cooperated with the group?
- What event caused the organization to collapse?
- Which characters changed allegiance?

## Media Rules

Suitable media may include:

- Logos
- Buildings
- Uniforms
- Symbols
- Group photographs
- Headquarters
- Flags
- Sigils
- Branded products

The media must not display the complete organization name when the question asks for it.

## Validation Rules

Reject the question if:

- The organization is never officially named.
- The group exists only in fan terminology.
- Membership changes make the question ambiguous without a timeframe.
- Multiple organizations share the same symbol.
- The media displays the answer directly.
- The question mixes organizations from different series.
- Multiple answers are reasonably possible.

When membership or leadership changes, specify the relevant season, episode, or period.
