# Visual Concept Rules

## One dominant meaning

Each image must communicate one intended Arabic word immediately.

For every visual part define:

- intendedArabicWord
- visualConcept
- alternativeArabicNames
- ambiguityRisk

## Acceptance rule

Use a concept only when its intended Arabic name is substantially more obvious than its alternatives.

Good examples:

- باب
- عين
- نار
- تاج
- مفتاح
- وردة
- قمر
- ساعة
- سم
- بوسة

Risky examples:

- complex emotional scenes,
- abstract ideas,
- generic landscapes,
- objects with many equally common names,
- actions that are difficult to identify from a still image.

## Ambiguity

Reject an image concept when players may reasonably call it by a different word that breaks the puzzle.

Example:

A water image may be called:

- بحر
- ماء
- موج
- محيط

Use it only when the composition clearly communicates the intended word.

## Human actions

Human actions must be unmistakable.

For بوسة:

- face visible,
- lips clearly puckered,
- kissing gesture obvious,
- no unrelated people or clutter.

## Image clarity

Every image must have:

- one dominant subject,
- large subject scale,
- clear contrast,
- simple background,
- no captions,
- no visible answer,
- no watermark,
- no unnecessary objects.
