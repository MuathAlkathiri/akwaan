# Wigolo Research Tool Contract

Use local cache search before live web access. Research only claims and assets
required by the selected ChallengeType, Pattern, World, and Scope. Preserve
source URL, publisher, access date, claim, confidence, spoiler or version
boundary, and source ID.

Prefer official primary sources. Use independent corroboration for disputed
claims. Search results, filenames, and snippets are discovery aids, not final
evidence. Never let a research tool choose the mechanic, Pattern, payload, or
player interaction.
