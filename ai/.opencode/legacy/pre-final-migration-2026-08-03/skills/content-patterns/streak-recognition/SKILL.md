# KILLSTREAK_RECOGNITION / SCORESTREAK_RECOGNITION

Recognize a named streak reward from a distinctive activation, announcer cue,
vehicle, strike, icon, effect, or bounded gameplay clip. Prefer audio when it is
uniquely recognizable. Verify the title-specific official name and whether the
game uses killstreak or scorestreak terminology. Reject answer-spoken clips,
generic explosions, wrong-title attribution, and approximate spellings as the
canonical answer.

Arabic example: `ما اسم هذا الكيلستريك من الصوت؟`
