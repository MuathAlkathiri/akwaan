# TV Series Validation

Reject the question if:

- Multiple answers are possible.
- The media contains multiple important characters.
- The screenshot is blurry.
- The quote belongs to multiple characters.
- The answer depends on personal opinion.
- The scene requires context from another scene.
- The accessibility and recognition load do not fit the intended audience.
- The answer is not visible enough.
- The question contains spoilers in broad-audience accessibility.
- The Media is decorative or missing as a readable local file.
- Media and text independently reveal the same identity.
- It asks an obvious Direct Relationship.
- It uses an obvious character for Direct Character Identification.
- It defaults to actor, production, date, award, or episode-count trivia.

Accept only questions with one obvious answer.
