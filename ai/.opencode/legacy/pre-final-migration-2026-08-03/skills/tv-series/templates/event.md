# Event Questions

## Purpose

Design trivia questions about important events that occur within a television series.

## Preferred Question Styles

- ما اسم هذا الحدث؟
- ماذا حدث في هذا المشهد؟
- ما الحدث الذي أدى إلى هذه النتيجة؟
- من تسبب في هذا الحدث؟
- أين وقع هذا الحدث؟
- في أي موسم وقع هذا الحدث؟

## Suitable Events

- Battles
- Weddings
- Escapes
- Arrests
- Betrayals
- Executions
- Disappearances
- Major discoveries
- Changes of leadership
- Important confrontations
- Turning points in the story

## Accessibility calibration

### Easy

Use highly recognizable events that are central to the series.

Examples:

- Famous battles
- Iconic confrontations
- Major character transformations
- Well-known deaths

### Medium

Use important events involving supporting characters or secondary storylines.

Examples:

- Betrayals
- Investigations
- Escapes
- Political decisions
- Business conflicts

### Hard

Use episode-specific events or less memorable consequences.

Examples:

- Minor confrontations
- Specific plans
- Hidden agreements
- Events involving secondary characters
- Causes or consequences of complex events

## Media Rules

The selected image or video must:

- Clearly represent the intended event.
- Show a recognizable moment from the event.
- Avoid revealing the answer directly through subtitles or text.
- Avoid showing multiple unrelated events.
- Contain enough visual context to answer the question.

## Validation Rules

Reject the question if:

- The event has no official name and the expected answer is unclear.
- Multiple events could match the media.
- The answer requires knowledge not shown or mentioned in the question.
- The wording asks for an opinion or interpretation.
- The event exists only in books, fan theories, or deleted scenes.
- The media reveals the written answer directly.
- The question has more than one reasonable answer.

The final question must have one clear and verifiable answer.
