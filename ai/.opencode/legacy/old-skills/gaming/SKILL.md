## Identity

Video Games questions test player memory, not industry trivia. Players remember
maps, levels, objectives, modes, weapons, items, equipment, abilities, vehicles,
HUD/UI, icons, sounds, music cues, characters, factions, missions, bosses,
strategies, and match moments. Prefer the experience of playing: recognizing a
space, mechanic, sound, interface, action, or consequence.

## Pattern and Gameplay Priorities

Choose a Question Pattern before an entity. Favor map/level recognition, weapon
or item recognition, distinctive sound recognition, title recognition from
HUD/UI or handling, mode/objective inference, ability/mechanic recognition,
mission/event recall, action attribution, boss or match outcomes, and limited
character identification. Pattern diversity and gameplay diversity must both
pass: different weapons do not make repeated `image -> name weapon` gameplay
varied.

## Media and Difficulty

Prefer real gameplay screenshots and bounded gameplay clips, then distinctive
sound effects or music. Text is appropriate for meaningful mechanics or lore.
Do not force audio or video where the game lacks a strong Asset. Difficulty
comes from player familiarity, prominence, attention, mechanical depth, and
fair scene recall. Easy means familiar but not immediately exposed by a hero,
logo, title label, loading screen, or answer-bearing UI. Hard never means an
ambiguous sound, unreadable crop, obscure production fact, or poor Asset.

Gameplay Media must be canonical to the correct title/version and necessary to
answer. Prefer clean first-person/in-engine captures and original game audio;
reject custom/modded content presented as canonical, thumbnails or overlays
containing the answer, fan edits, montages, reactions, decorative Media, and
remote-only Assets. Every selected image/audio/video must be downloaded,
openable, and validated locally before completion.

Reject release dates, developers, publishers, sales, awards, production facts,
selection-screen or promotional identity giveaways, Direct Relationship, and
decorative Media unless the Subject explicitly targets industry knowledge.
