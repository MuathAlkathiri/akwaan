# General Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Material is everyday shared knowledge: countries and capitals, famous
landmarks, continents and seas, common foods, well-known sports, science
basics, and widely known cultural markers. Use short Arabic phrasing with
verified English or romanized aliases.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Countries, cities, capitals, seas, rivers, and landmarks with one stable
  form.
- Everyday foods, drinks, and dishes known across the region.
- Well-known sports, teams, and major tournament facts.
- Common science basics and widely taught facts that are not debated.
- Famous cultural symbols and shared popular-culture basics.

## Stable Relationships and Classifications

Country-to-capital, landmark-to-city, sport-to-country, food-to-region, and
item-to-material links are useful only when the pairing has one agreed answer.

## Numeric Dimensions

Counts, dates, sizes, and rankings change or get disputed; prefer a fact that
is stable and broadly agreed. Never use a live or unlabeled value.

## Media Anchors

Flags, maps, silhouettes, food plates, sports equipment, and landmark images.
Remove text, names, watermarks, and any visible label that leaks the answer.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Translation, regional naming, and disputed values are common risks. Prefer
broad, stable, widely agreed facts; record a dated source with every claim the
team must trust.
