# Letters and Words

## Identity and Vocabulary

Puzzle material is the Arabic alphabet and everyday words: letter tiles,
syllables, common roots, word parts, and simple word relationships. Use short,
common, unambiguous Arabic words with verified spelling.

## Safe Entity Sets

- The 28 Arabic letters in their standard isolated forms.
- Short everyday words with one clear spelling.
- Common syllables and prefixes that combine predictably.
- Simple word pairs: opposites, synonyms, and compound words.
- Small sets of words that differ by one letter.

## Stable Relationships and Classifications

Letter-to-sound, letter-to-position, word-to-meaning, opposite-to-opposite, and
compound-word links are stable when the pair has one agreed answer.

## Numeric Dimensions

Letter counts, word lengths, and position numbers are the main numeric axes;
they must match the exact written form used in the fragments.

## Media Anchors

Letter tiles, word cards, syllable blocks, and grids. Remove any label or shape
that reveals the target word.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Hamza forms, taa marbuta, and diacritics cause spelling disputes. Prefer common
words whose plain spelling has one accepted form and record that form exactly.
