# Scope Knowledge Profile: numbers-estimation

## Identity / Boundaries
أرقام وتقديرات — Playful numerical intuition. numerical intuition a player applies in seconds. Arithmetic may appear, but the puzzle is the insight, never the computation.

## Strong Content Territories
Strong: sequences with a noticeable rule, quantity comparisons, proportion-at-a-glance, counting under a trick, numeric patterns with one defensible continuation.

## Weak / Boring Territories
Weak: long multiplication, anything a calculator makes trivial, sequences with two defensible continuations.

## Evidence Opportunities
number tiles, quantity grids, proportion diagrams.

## Terminology / Aliases
Use the player-facing Arabic name **أرقام وتقديرات** in copy; use the canonical slug
`numbers-estimation` in every technical identifier. No other name or slug is approved.

## Ambiguity Traps
a sequence that admits a second valid rule; an 'estimate' with no authored tolerance.

## Scope-Specific Taste
A good item here makes the player say «آه، شفتها» — the satisfaction comes from
the solve itself, never from having known something. Every item must survive
both `PUZZLES_WORLD_NO_TRIVIA` and `PUZZLE_ITSELF_IS_THE_CHALLENGE`.
