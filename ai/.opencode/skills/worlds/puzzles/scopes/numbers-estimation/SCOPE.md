# Scope: Numbers and Estimation (أرقام وتقديرات)

- `scopeId`: `puzzles.numbers-estimation`
- `worldId`: `puzzles`
- Player-facing Arabic name: **أرقام وتقديرات**
- Primary solve: **Playful numerical intuition.**
- The question it asks the player: «What does your sense of quantity say?»
- Boundary: numerical intuition a player applies in seconds. Arithmetic may appear, but
  the puzzle is the insight, never the computation.
- Included: quantity, proportion, counting, numeric patterns, time, light calculation.
- Excluded: **school-math worksheet content** (§23.11, explicit), multi-step arithmetic,
  anything needing a calculator or paper, and formula recall.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []

## World invariants this Scope inherits

- `PUZZLES_WORLD_NO_TRIVIA` — ordinary trivia in a cosmetic puzzle wrapper is
  invalid here. An item that a player answers by *knowing a fact* does not
  belong in this Scope regardless of how it is dressed.
- `PUZZLE_ITSELF_IS_THE_CHALLENGE` — the puzzle must be self-contained in what
  the player is shown. No outside lookup, no recall dependency.

## Safety

No real-person names or likenesses, no brands, no money, body shape or weight,
religion, romantic relationships, intelligence judgements, or awkward
family-group topics. Keep to neutral, everyday material.
