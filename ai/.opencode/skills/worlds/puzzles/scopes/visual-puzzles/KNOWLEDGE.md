## Scope Summary
Engages the visual-spatial cortex of players through geometric deduction, mental folding, optical decomposition, shape counting, and visual pattern recognition.

## Key Territories & Visual Puzzle Types
- **Shape Decomposition & Intersection Counting:** How many total triangles exist inside an equilateral triangle divided by lines from vertices? How many squares on an 8x8 chessboard of all sizes (1x1 to 8x8 = 204 squares)? How many cubes in a 3D isometric stack with hidden support blocks?
- **Spatial Folding & Cube Nets:** Opposite faces on standard 6-sided dice (always sum to 7: 1 opposite 6, 2 opposite 5, 3 opposite 4), unfolded cube net patterns where players determine which side is opposite another.
- **Mental Rotation & Symmetry:** Clock hands angle calculations (angle at 3:15 is 7.5 degrees, angle at 6:00 is 180 degrees), reflection across axes, rotational symmetry orders of polygons (regular pentagon, hexagon, octagon).
- **Color, Shade & Venn Visual Logic:** Overlapping colored filters (subtractive/additive primary colors: Red+Green+Blue = White light, Cyan+Magenta+Yellow = Black pigment), Venn diagram region arithmetic.
- **Optical & Geometry Theorems:** Tangram shapes, polygon diagonal counts formula [n*(n-3)/2], Euler's polyhedron formula (Vertices - Edges + Faces = 2).

## Quality & Calibration Guidelines
- Questions in text-only mode must describe the spatial figure with absolute mathematical precision so the player can visualize the exact shape in their mind.
- For image-supported mechanics (Bomb), the media intent must specify clean, high-contrast, uncluttered diagrams.
