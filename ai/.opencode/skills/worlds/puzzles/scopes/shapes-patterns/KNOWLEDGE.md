# Shapes and Patterns

## Identity and Vocabulary

Puzzle material is visual: shapes, figure sequences, pattern repeats, matrices,
symmetry, rotation, and simple spatial logic. Shapes must be describable aloud:
colors, sides, corners, size, and direction.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Basic shapes: square, circle, triangle, and rectangle in clear sizes.
- Simple figure sequences with one repeat rule.
- Small matrices of shapes that complete a row or column.
- Symmetry, rotation, and reflection of simple figures.
- Countable shapes inside one figure.

## Stable Relationships and Classifications

Shape-to-shape links (next in sequence, completing the matrix, rotated or
reflected copy) are stable when the figure has one clear reading.

## Numeric Dimensions

Sides, corners, counts, and order positions are the main numeric axes; they must
match what the fragments actually show.

## Media Anchors

Shape cards, figure strips, matrix grids, and pattern tiles. Remove any label
that names the final figure.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Shading, orientation, and figure scale are common risks. Fix colors and forms in
the fragments so two players cannot describe them differently.
