# Scope Knowledge Profile: paths-motion

## Identity / Boundaries
مسارات وحركة — Predict a deterministic system in motion. a closed, fully specified mechanism whose outcome follows necessarily from what is drawn. Determinism is the contract: one state in, one state out.

## Strong Content Territories
Strong: gear-direction chains, which bulb lights, which rope lifts, domino cascades, tangled paths, switch logic.

## Weak / Boring Territories
Weak: systems with too many stages to trace on a phone, anything needing real physics, diagrams whose crossings are ambiguous at render size.

## Evidence Opportunities
mechanism diagrams with unambiguous crossings, clear connection points and legible direction markers.

## Terminology / Aliases
Use the player-facing Arabic name **مسارات وحركة** in copy; use the canonical slug
`paths-motion` in every technical identifier. No other name or slug is approved.

## Ambiguity Traps
a crossing that reads either way at phone size; a mechanism whose stated outcome needs an unstated assumption.

## Scope-Specific Taste
A good item here makes the player say «آه، شفتها» — the satisfaction comes from
the solve itself, never from having known something. Every item must survive
both `PUZZLES_WORLD_NO_TRIVIA` and `PUZZLE_ITSELF_IS_THE_CHALLENGE`.
