# Lateral Thinking Knowledge Base (قاعدة معرفة تفكير جانبي)

## Scope Summary
Challenges players to solve constrained scenarios by challenging hidden cognitive assumptions and discovering the single logical reality that harmonizes all clues.

## Key Territories & Puzzle Types
- **Assumption Reframing Scenarios:** Classic logical situations where gender, profession, environment, or perspective is misassumed (e.g. doctors/surgeons, day vs night, twins born across timezones/leap years, elevators stopped by raincoats/umbrellas).
- **Physical & Spatial Logic:** Weight and balance paradoxes, crossing rivers with logical constraints, candle and lighting problems, containers and liquid transfers with strict volume bounds.
- **Truth & Deduction Scenarios:** Statements with precise linguistic qualifiers (e.g. "always", "at least one", "neither", "all but five survive"), binary truth/lie deduction logic puzzles.
- **Unexpected But Defensible Constraints:** Scenarios involving calendar quirks (leap years, February, months with 28 vs 31 days), family relationships across 3 generations (2 fathers and 2 sons = grandfather, father, son).

## Quality & Calibration Guidelines
- Every puzzle must be solvable from the prompt alone without outside trivia.
- The solution must feel instantly obvious, fair, and indisputable once revealed ("Aha! Of course!").
- Prompts must be concise in Arabic (2-3 lines maximum).
