### Legacy Taxonomy Data:
# Logic and Deduction

## Identity and Vocabulary

Puzzle material is classic deduction: clues, ordering rules, matching links, and
true/false statements that the team reasons through aloud. Keep variables few
and names neutral.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Neutral actors: colors, shapes, objects, pets, fruits, and vehicles.
- Small ordered sets: three or four positions, steps, or ranks.
- Matching pairs with one consistent rule.
- Simple true/false statements with one clear reading.
- Constraint sets the team can hold in mind without notes.

## Stable Relationships and Classifications

If-then links, ordering, and matching are stable when each rule has one reading
and no hidden case.

## Numeric Dimensions

Positions, counts, and steps must be exact and stated clearly; a single wrong
number breaks the deduction.

## Media Anchors

Order strips, matching lines, cards, and small grids. Remove any label that
reveals the final arrangement.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Rule wording, ordering direction, and implied assumptions are common risks.
Write rules so each statement has exactly one meaning.
