# Patterns & Sequences Knowledge Base (قاعدة معرفة أنماط وتسلسل)

## Scope Summary
Dedicated to mathematical, geometric, and logical sequence discovery. Every item presents a clear sequence or pattern where players deduce the governing algorithmic rule to find the missing element or next step.

## Key Territories & Sequence Types
- **Arithmetic & Geometric Variations:** Step progressions with variable increments (e.g. +2, +4, +8, +16; alternating +3, -1, +3, -1; multiplying then subtracting).
- **Famous Mathematical Sequences:** Fibonacci sequence (1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21...), Prime number sequences (2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23...), Perfect squares (1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100...), Perfect cubes (1, 8, 27, 64, 125...).
- **Structural & Positional Cycles:** Clock arithmetic patterns (modular arithmetic mod 12, mod 24, mod 7 for days of the week), repeating symbol transformations, matrix row/column progression rules.
- **Double & Interleaved Sequences:** Two independent sequences woven together in alternating odd/even positions (e.g. 2, 20, 4, 18, 6, 16, [8], [14]).
- **Digital & Visual Transformations:** Digital root sums, symmetry patterns, letter-position progressions based on alphabetical indices or geometric line counts.

## Quality & Calibration Guidelines
- Keep the number of given elements sufficient (minimum 4 elements) so the sequence has exactly one unambiguous mathematical/logical continuation.
- Avoid hyper-complex polynomials that require calculus; focus on elegant, instantly satisfying "aha!" rules.
