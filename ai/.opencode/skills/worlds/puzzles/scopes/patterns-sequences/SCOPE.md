# Scope: Patterns & Sequences (أنماط وتسلسل)

- `scopeId`: `puzzles.patterns-sequences`
- `worldId`: `puzzles`
- Boundary: Rule-governed sequences, pattern deduction, progressive orderings, geometric progressions, Fibonacci/prime steps, repeating structural cycles, and logical transformations.
- Included: Number sequences where the challenge is discovering the hidden rule (e.g. alternating operations, square/cube offsets, digital root patterns), word/letter progression rules, visual-rule transformations expressed in text/data, and ordering deductions.
- Excluded: Direct arithmetic drills without a pattern rule (which belongs to "أرقام وحساب"), vocabulary word anagrams (which belongs to "حروف وكلمات"), cultural trivia.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Principle: The challenge must be finding the governing rule of the progression, not performing tedious calculations.
