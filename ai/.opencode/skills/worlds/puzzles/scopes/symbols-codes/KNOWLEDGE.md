### Legacy Taxonomy Data:
# Symbols and Codes

## Identity and Vocabulary

Puzzle material is symbols and codes: a mapping or substitution rule the team
applies to decode one message. Keep keys small so two players can hold them in
mind and say them aloud.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Simple symbols: stars, circles, squares, arrows, and digits.
- Letter-to-number and letter-to-symbol maps with one rule.
- Short hidden words and phrases in everyday Arabic.
- Substitution rules with one clear direction.
- Key material split so no single fragment holds the full code.

## Stable Relationships and Classifications

Symbol-to-letter, number-to-letter, and rule-to-output links are stable when the
key is complete across the fragments and the direction is stated.

## Numeric Dimensions

Positions and values in a code must be exact; a single wrong digit or offset
breaks the decode.

## Media Anchors

Code cards, symbol grids, and key strips. Remove any label that spells out the
hidden message.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Mapping direction, zero-based versus one-based positions, and ambiguous symbols
are common risks. State the direction in the instruction and keep symbols
visually distinct.
