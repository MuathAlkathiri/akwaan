# Scope Knowledge Profile: assembly-rotation

## Identity / Boundaries
تركيب وتدوير — Spatial transformation of the object itself. the object is transformed in the player's head and the answer is the result of that transformation.

## Strong Content Territories
Strong: cube nets, rotated shapes, silhouettes from an unusual angle, fold/unfold, which piece completes the figure, cross-sections.

## Weak / Boring Territories
Weak: transformations too fine to read on a phone, shapes whose answer depends on exact colour reproduction, anything needing real 3D manipulation.

## Evidence Opportunities
clean line figures, nets, projection sets and piece trays with consistent scale and orientation cues.

## Terminology / Aliases
Use the player-facing Arabic name **تركيب وتدوير** in copy; use the canonical slug
`assembly-rotation` in every technical identifier. No other name or slug is approved.

## Ambiguity Traps
a rotation with an accidental symmetry making two options correct; a net that folds into the target more than one way.

## Scope-Specific Taste
A good item here makes the player say «آه، شفتها» — the satisfaction comes from
the solve itself, never from having known something. Every item must survive
both `PUZZLES_WORLD_NO_TRIVIA` and `PUZZLE_ITSELF_IS_THE_CHALLENGE`.
