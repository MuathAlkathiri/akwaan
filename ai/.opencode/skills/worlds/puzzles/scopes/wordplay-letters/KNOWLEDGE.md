# Scope Knowledge Profile: wordplay-letters

## Identity / Boundaries
التلاعب بالكلمات والحروف — Language itself is the puzzle. the Arabic script and the words built from it are the material being manipulated, not merely the medium the question is written in.

## Strong Content Territories
Strong: anagrams of common words, palindromes, words hidden inside longer words, one-letter substitutions that change meaning, letter-position patterns.

## Weak / Boring Territories
Weak: rare words, disputed spellings, items that hinge on diacritics or hamza forms that render unreliably, anything needing a dictionary.

## Evidence Opportunities
letter tiles, word cards and scrambled-letter strips; the rendered form must match the intended spelling exactly.

## Terminology / Aliases
Use the player-facing Arabic name **التلاعب بالكلمات والحروف** in copy; use the canonical slug
`wordplay-letters` in every technical identifier. No other name or slug is approved.

## Ambiguity Traps
hamza and taa-marbuta variants with more than one accepted spelling; anagrams with a second valid solution the author did not notice.

## Scope-Specific Taste
A good item here makes the player say «آه، شفتها» — the satisfaction comes from
the solve itself, never from having known something. Every item must survive
both `PUZZLES_WORLD_NO_TRIVIA` and `PUZZLE_ITSELF_IS_THE_CHALLENGE`.
