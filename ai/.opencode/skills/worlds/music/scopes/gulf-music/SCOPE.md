# Scope: Gulf Music

- `scopeId`: `music.gulf-music`
- `worldId`: `music`
- Boundary: Recognizable music and artists from Kuwait, UAE, Bahrain, Qatar,
  Oman, and the wider Gulf scene — excluding Saudi artists who belong to the
  dedicated Saudi Music scope.
- Included: recognizable Gulf artists, famous songs, musical identity,
  collaborations, eras, artist/song recognition, and famous albums.
- Excluded: Saudi-heavy pools (Saudi Music has its own scope); volatile
  streaming/chart totals; ambiguous-attribution songs.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Pattern exclusions: lyric-fragment content items beyond fair short fragments.
- Safety: keep geographic and artist diversity across the Gulf states. Arabic
  artist names with verified romanized aliases.
