# Scope: Gulf Music

- `scopeId`: `music.gulf-music`
- `worldId`: `music`
- Boundary: Recognizable music and artists from Kuwait, UAE, Bahrain, Qatar,
  Oman, and the wider Gulf scene — excluding Saudi artists who belong to the
  dedicated Saudi Music scope.
- Included: recognizable Gulf artists, famous songs, musical identity,
  collaborations, eras, artist/song recognition, and famous albums.
- Excluded: Saudi-heavy pools (Saudi Music has its own scope); volatile
  streaming/chart totals; ambiguous-attribution songs.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Pattern exclusions: lyric-fragment content items beyond fair short fragments.
- Safety: keep geographic and artist diversity across the Gulf states. Arabic
  artist names with verified romanized aliases (نبيل شعيل, حسين الجسمي,
  عيضة المنهالي).

# Gulf Music Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Durable sets include flagship Gulf artists per country, their signature songs,
and regional musical identity.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Kuwait: نبيل شعيل, عبدالله الرويشد, نوال الكويتية.
- UAE: حسين الجسمي, أحلام, عيضة المنهالي, بلقيس.
- Bahrain: خالد الشيخ, عبدالله المطيري.
- Qatar: فهد الكبيسي.
- Oman: صالح زعل.
- Signature songs and albums widely recognizable and stably attributed.

## Stable Classifications

Artist-to-country, artist-to-song, and artist-to-album associations are
deterministic and safe for flagship material.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Streaming and chart statistics, contested song attribution, and lyric
ownership are high risk. Prefer official artist material and licensed
reference data; date-bound any time-sensitive fact. Arabic fandom spellings
must match established usage.
