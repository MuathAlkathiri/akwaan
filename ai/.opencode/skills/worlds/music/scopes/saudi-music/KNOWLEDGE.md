# Saudi Music Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Durable sets include flagship artists, their signature songs and albums,
long-running careers and eras, and famous collaborations.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Artists: محمد عبده, عبدالمجيد عبدالله, عبادي الجوهر, رابح صقر, خالد
  عبدالرحمن, راشد الماجد, طلال مداح, محمد عبده's iconic repertoire.
- Signature songs and albums that are widely recognizable and stably
  attributed.
- Eras and career-spanning facts (e.g. decades of career, genre identity).
- Famous collaborations between major Saudi artists.

## Stable Classifications

Artist-to-song, artist-to-album, and artist-to-genre associations are
deterministic and safe for flagship material.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Streaming and chart statistics, contested song attribution, and lyric
ownership are high risk. Prefer official artist material and licensed
reference data; date-bound any time-sensitive fact. Arabic fandom spellings
must match established usage.
