# Scope: Arabic Music

- `scopeId`: `music.arabic-music`
- `worldId`: `music`
- Boundary: Major recognizable music and artists from Egypt, Lebanon, Syria,
  North Africa, and the wider Arab music scene.
- Included: recognizable Arab artists, famous songs, musical identity,
  collaborations, eras, artist/song recognition, and famous albums.
- Excluded: Saudi and Gulf artists who belong to their dedicated scopes;
  volatile streaming/chart totals; obscure archival trivia.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Pattern exclusions: lyric-fragment content items beyond fair short fragments.
- Safety: maintain geographic and artist diversity — no one country or singer
  may dominate. Arabic artist names with verified romanized aliases
  (أم كلثوم, عبدالحليم حافظ, فيروز, عمرو دياب, شيرين).

# Arabic Music Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Durable sets include flagship Arab artists per country, their signature songs,
and the classic vs modern eras of Arab music.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Egypt: أم كلثوم, عبدالحليم حافظ, محمد عبدالوهاب, عمرو دياب, شيرين,
  تامر حسني, أنغام.
- Lebanon: فيروز, وديع الصافي, ماجدة الرومي, نانسي عجرم, إليسا.
- Syria: جورج وسوف, أصالة, فارس كرم.
- North Africa: الشاب خالد (Algeria), لطيفة (Tunisia), سعد لمجرد
  (Morocco).
- Signature songs, albums, and famous collaborations widely recognizable.

## Stable Classifications

Artist-to-country, artist-to-song, and artist-to-album associations are
deterministic and safe for flagship material.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Streaming and chart statistics, contested song attribution, and lyric
ownership are high risk. Prefer official artist material and licensed
reference data; date-bound any time-sensitive fact. Arabic fandom spellings
must match established usage.
