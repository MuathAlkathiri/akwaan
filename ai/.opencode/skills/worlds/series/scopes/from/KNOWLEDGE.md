## Identity and Vocabulary

Durable sets include main characters, the town and its rules, the surrounding
forest, the nighttime creatures, talismans, and central mysteries.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Main characters (Boyd Stevens, Jim, Tabitha, Donna, Father Khatri, Sara,
  Julie, Ethan, Jade, Kristi).
- The town rules and mechanics (talismans, the monster rules at night, the
  tree/road that leads to town, the box).
- Locations (the town, the forest, the lighthouse, the tunnels, the church).

## Stable Classifications

Character role and town-mechanic facts are usable when tied to the aired
seasons and stated without resolving the central mystery.

## Ambiguity and Sources

The mystery's unresolved explanations, fan theories, and future-season reveals
are high risk. Prefer MGM+ official material and licensed reference data.
Record a spoiler endpoint with every unstable claim. Arabic fandom spellings
must match established usage.
