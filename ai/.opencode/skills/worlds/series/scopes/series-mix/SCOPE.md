# Scope: Series Mix

- `scopeId`: `series.series-mix`
- `worldId`: `series`
- Boundary: Controlled incubation pool of established series:
  The Walking Dead, Dexter, Prison Break, Lost, The Boys, House of the Dragon,
  Peaky Blinders, Stranger Things.
- Included: series-level facts, flagship characters, and signature locations
  that are unambiguous within each show's established run.
- Excluded: niche deep-cut lore from a single show; anything requiring
  franchise-specific insider knowledge to answer.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Pattern exclusions: cross-show comparisons without a named series.
- Safety: each item must name or clearly imply its series in the prompt;
  avoid spoilers and late-series twists. Arabic names with verified romanized
  aliases.
