# Series Mix Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Mixed series pool: each item must be answerable from one flagship fact about
the named show — the show itself is part of the answer space.

## Safe Entity Sets

- The Walking Dead: Rick Grimes, the group, walkers, Alexandria.
- Dexter: Dexter Morgan, blood-spatter analyst, "Dark Passenger", Miami.
- Prison Break: Michael Scofield, Lincoln Burrows, the escape, Fox River.
- Lost: island survivors, the hatch, Oceanic Flight 815, Jack/Charlie.
- The Boys: Butcher, Homelander, the Seven, Compound V.
- House of the Dragon: House Targaryen, Daemon, Rhaenyra, dragons.
- Peaky Blinders: Thomas Shelby, the family gang, Birmingham, the razor caps.
- Stranger Things: Eleven, Hawkins, the Upside Down, the Demogorgon.

## Stable Classifications

Show-to-character/show-to-location associations are deterministic and safe.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Deep lore, cross-show confusion, and spoilers are high risk. Prefer each
show's official material. Record a spoiler endpoint with every unstable claim.
