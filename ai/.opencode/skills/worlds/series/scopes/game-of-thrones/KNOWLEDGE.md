# Game of Thrones Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Durable sets include noble houses, sigils, mottos, major characters, capital
locations, geographic regions, titles, dragons, and named wars.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Major houses and their sigils/mottos (Stark, Lannister, Targaryen, Baratheon,
  Greyjoy, Tully, Arryn, Martell, Tyrell).
- Central characters across the series and their stable roles.
- Capitals and landmark locations (King's Landing, Winterfell, The Wall, etc.).
- Dragons, named battles, and political institutions within the boundary.

## Stable Classifications

House affiliation, title, location, and alliance are usable when tied to the
series' established era (Seasons 1–8).

## Ambiguity and Sources

Transliteration variants, character deaths, identity reveals, and shifting
alliances are high risk. Prefer HBO official material and licensed reference
data. Record a spoiler endpoint with every unstable claim. Arabic fandom
spellings must match established usage (e.g. "جون سنو" not "جون سنوز").
