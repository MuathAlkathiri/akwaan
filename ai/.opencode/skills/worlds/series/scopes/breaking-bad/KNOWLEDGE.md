# Breaking Bad Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Durable sets include main characters, secondary characters, locations, the
chemistry/drug-trade plot, law enforcement, and key events.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Main characters (Walter White, Jesse Pinkman, Saul Goodman, Skyler, Hank,
  Gus Fring, Mike, Todd) and their stable roles.
- Landmark locations (Albuquerque, the desert, the RV, the lab, Los Pollos
  Hermanos, A1A Car Wash).
- Recurring items and devices (gas mask, hazmat suit, the fly, blue meth,
  "Say My Name").

## Stable Classifications

Character role/affiliation, location association, and named event are usable
when tied to the established series era.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Transliteration variants, character outcomes, and shifting alliances are high
risk. Prefer AMC official material and licensed reference data. Record a
spoiler endpoint with every unstable claim. Arabic fandom spellings must match
established usage (e.g. "والتر وايت" not "والتر ويت").
