# World: المسلسلات (Series)

- `worldId`: `series`
- Identity: scripted serialized fiction — characters, factions, locations,
  episodes, seasons, creators, and signature moments.
- Tone: narrative, character-led, suspenseful, and recognition-led.
- Media anchors: authentic series stills, cast frames, location shots, and
  bounded clips (spoiler-safe wording only).
- Safety: distinguish seasons and series continuity; never leak late-series
  twists, deaths, or identity reveals through prompts, clues, or metadata.
- Presentation: cinematic input framing, show-specific sound profile, and
  episode-reveal motion.
- Signature mechanic: unassigned; the World is not launch-ready.

This World supplies presentation context only.

## Question Palette

- **High-Value Archetypes**:
  - `WHO_SAID_OR_DID_IT` (Iconic character quotes: `"I am the one who knocks" -> Walter White`)
  - `REVERSE_QUESTION` (Actor -> TV character)
  - `CHARACTER_TO_WORK` (Distinctive side characters -> Show title)
  - `DETAIL_RECOGNITION` (Fictional companies, signature cars, iconic emblems)
  - `NAME_FRAGMENT` & `NICKNAME_OR_ALIAS` (Character aliases: `"Saul Goodman" -> Jimmy McGill`)
- **Usable Archetypes**:
  - `CONNECTION` (Shared families, rival factions)
  - `BEFORE_AFTER` (Season-level milestones)
  - `FAST_ATTRIBUTE` (Character professions, family relations)
- **Archetypes to Limit**:
  - Exact episode titles or timestamps.
  - Mid-season filler dialogue without cultural footprint.
- **Content Dimensions to Rotate Through**:
  - Lead Characters, Antagonists, Factions & Corporations, Signature Locations, Dramatic Turning Points.
- **World-Specific Anti-Patterns**:
  - *Twist Spoilers*: Never frame a question around a season finale murder or betrayal reveal if it ruins first-time viewer enjoyment.
