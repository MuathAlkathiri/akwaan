# NBA Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Durable sets include players, teams, championships, MVPs, and iconic moments.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Players: Michael Jordan, LeBron James, Kobe Bryant, Magic Johnson, Larry Bird,
  Shaquille O'Neal, Stephen Curry, Tim Duncan, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Wilt
  Chamberlain, Bill Russell, Kevin Durant.
- Teams: Lakers, Celtics, Bulls, Warriors, Heat, Spurs, Knicks, 76ers, Nuggets,
  Bucks.
- Championships/MVPs: Jordan 6 rings, Russell 11 rings, MJ 5 MVPs, Kareem 6 MVPs.
- Records: Wilt 100 points, most career points (LeBron), Russell 11 titles.

## Stable Classifications

Player-to-team, player-to-era, and player-to-accomplishment associations are
deterministic and safe for historical facts.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Current rosters, live totals, and current champions are high risk — date-bind
or avoid. Prefer stable historical facts. Maintain team/era/star diversity.
Arabic terms must match established usage.
