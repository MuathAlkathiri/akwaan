# Scope: Arab Music (نجوم الغناء العرب)

- `scopeId`: `celebrities.arab-music-stars`
- `worldId`: `celebrities`
- Boundary: Arab singers and musicians and their public careers. Exclusivity rule: a celebrity belongs to one Scope only. A singer who also acts belongs here when singing is the primary or initial source of fame.
- Included: Songs, albums, tours, collaborations, stage names, awards.
- Excluded: Anyone whose primary fame belongs to `arab-acting-stars`, `arab-sports-stars`, `arab-content-creators`, `arab-media-stars`; non-Arab celebrities, who belong to `non-arab-music-stars`; marriage, relationships, children and family, health, religion, politics, money, appearance, age, rumours and legal disputes.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Pattern exclusions: current status without an effective date, and unverified claims about a real person.
- Safety & Precision: Public career facts only. Anchor every fact to a named work, event or year.
