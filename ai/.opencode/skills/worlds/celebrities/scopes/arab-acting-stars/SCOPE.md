# Scope: Arab Acting (نجوم التمثيل العرب)

- `scopeId`: `celebrities.arab-acting-stars`
- `worldId`: `celebrities`
- Boundary: Arab actors and actresses and their public careers. Exclusivity rule: a celebrity belongs to one Scope only. An actor who also sings belongs here when acting is the primary or initial source of fame.
- Included: Film, television and stage work, famous roles, directors and co-stars, awards.
- Excluded: Anyone whose primary fame belongs to `arab-music-stars`, `arab-sports-stars`, `arab-content-creators`, `arab-media-stars`; non-Arab celebrities, who belong to `non-arab-acting-stars`; marriage, relationships, children and family, health, religion, politics, money, appearance, age, rumours and legal disputes.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Pattern exclusions: current status without an effective date, and unverified claims about a real person.
- Safety & Precision: Public career facts only. Anchor every fact to a named work, event or year.
