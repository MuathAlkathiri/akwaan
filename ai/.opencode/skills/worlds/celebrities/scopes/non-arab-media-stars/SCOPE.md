# Scope: Non-Arab TV and Media (نجوم التلفزيون والإعلام الأجانب)

- `scopeId`: `celebrities.non-arab-media-stars`
- `worldId`: `celebrities`
- Boundary: Non-Arab presenters, broadcasters and commentators and their public careers. Exclusivity rule: a celebrity belongs to one Scope only. A TV presenter with a social presence belongs here.
- Included: Programmes presented, broadcasting roles, famous interviews, signature catchphrases.
- Excluded: Anyone whose primary fame belongs to `non-arab-acting-stars`, `non-arab-music-stars`, `non-arab-sports-stars`, `non-arab-content-creators`; Arab celebrities, who belong to `arab-media-stars`; marriage, relationships, children and family, health, religion, politics, money, appearance, age, rumours and legal disputes.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Pattern exclusions: current status without an effective date, and unverified claims about a real person.
- Safety & Precision: Public career facts only. Anchor every fact to a named work, event or year.
