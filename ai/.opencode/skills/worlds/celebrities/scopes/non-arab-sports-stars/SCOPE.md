# Scope: Non-Arab Sports (نجوم الرياضة الأجانب)

- `scopeId`: `celebrities.non-arab-sports-stars`
- `worldId`: `celebrities`
- Boundary: Non-Arab athletes and their public careers. Exclusivity rule: a celebrity belongs to one Scope only. An athlete with a major social following still belongs here.
- Included: The sport, teams represented, titles, famous moments, public nicknames.
- Excluded: Anyone whose primary fame belongs to `non-arab-acting-stars`, `non-arab-music-stars`, `non-arab-content-creators`, `non-arab-media-stars`; Arab celebrities, who belong to `arab-sports-stars`; marriage, relationships, children and family, health, religion, politics, money, appearance, age, rumours and legal disputes.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Pattern exclusions: current status without an effective date, and unverified claims about a real person.
- Safety & Precision: Public career facts only. Anchor every fact to a named work, event or year.
