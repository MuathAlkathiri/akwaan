# Scope Knowledge Profile: arab-sports-stars

## Identity / Boundaries
Arab athletes, known here through their public work. Placement follows the exclusivity rule: a celebrity belongs to one Scope only.

## Experience-Native Knowledge
What audiences know from following this work, not from reading biographies.

## Strong Content Territories
Strong: Signature moments, public nicknames, titles, and the career eras fans remember.

## Weak / Boring Territories
Weak: Exact statistics, transfer fees, and season-by-season figures.

## Evidence Opportunities
Dated reporting and official pages for facts. Real photographs and recordings from the established media workflow.

## Terminology / Aliases
Use the name the public uses, in Arabic and English, and accept common transliterations.

## Ambiguity Traps
- Check the primary source of fame before placing someone: an athlete with a major social following still belongs here.
- Names can be shared or spelled in several ways, so confirm it is the same person.
- A work may have different titles in Arabic and English.

## Scope-Specific Taste
Questions must reward knowing the work, not knowing the person's private life.

## Volatile Facts
Everything below is stable. Current projects, teams, shows, awards, follower counts and records all change: research them with a dated source before use. None of those are stated here on purpose.
