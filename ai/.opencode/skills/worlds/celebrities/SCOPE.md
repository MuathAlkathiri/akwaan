# Celebrities (عالم المشاهير)

## Active Scopes (APPROVED TAXONOMY - 10 SCOPES)
1. نجوم التمثيل العرب (`arab-acting-stars`)
2. نجوم التمثيل الأجانب (`non-arab-acting-stars`)
3. نجوم الغناء العرب (`arab-music-stars`)
4. نجوم الغناء الأجانب (`non-arab-music-stars`)
5. نجوم الرياضة العرب (`arab-sports-stars`)
6. نجوم الرياضة الأجانب (`non-arab-sports-stars`)
7. صناع المحتوى العرب (`arab-content-creators`)
8. صناع المحتوى الأجانب (`non-arab-content-creators`)
9. نجوم التلفزيون والإعلام العرب (`arab-media-stars`)
10. نجوم التلفزيون والإعلام الأجانب (`non-arab-media-stars`)

## Exclusivity Rule
A celebrity belongs to **ONE Scope** according to two axes:

**Axis 1: Arab vs Non-Arab Identity/Context**
- Saudi, Gulf, Egyptian, Levantine, Maghrebi, etc. celebrities belong inside the appropriate ARAB Scope.
- Do NOT create separate geography Scopes by country at this stage.

**Axis 2: Primary Source of Fame**
- **Actor + Singer**: Assign based on their primary or initial source of fame.
- **Athlete with major social following**: `*-sports-stars`.
- **TV presenter with social presence**: `*-media-stars`.
- **Content creator who later appears on TV**: `*-content-creators` (unless their primary fame clearly shifted).

*No duplicate celebrity across Scopes is allowed.*
