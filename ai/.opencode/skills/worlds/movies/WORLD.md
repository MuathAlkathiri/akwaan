# World: الأفلام (Movies)

- `worldId`: `movies`
- Identity: cinematic, recognizable, fandom-driven films and franchises —
  characters, locations, objects, weapons, powers, factions, relationships,
  scenes, plot events, villains, heroes, and fictional worlds.
- Tone: cinematic, iconic, recognition-led, and discussion-friendly.
- Media anchors: authentic film stills, character frames, location shots, and
  bounded clips (spoiler-safe wording only).
- Safety: spoiler-aware like Series; respect the project's spoiler policy.
  Prefer movie-franchise canon over book/comics canon unless the Scope
  KNOWLEDGE.md explicitly includes it. Never reveal late-film twists, deaths,
  or identity reveals through prompts, clues, or metadata.
- Presentation: cinematic input framing, film-score sound profile, and
  scene-reveal motion.
- Signature mechanic: unassigned; the World is not launch-ready.

This World supplies presentation context only.

## Question Palette

- **High-Value Archetypes**:
  - `REVERSE_QUESTION` (Actor -> Iconic role / Oscar-winning character)
  - `REAL_NAME` (Superhero secret identities: `"Batman" -> Bruce Wayne`)
  - `CHARACTER_TO_WORK` & `WORK_TO_CHARACTER` (Franchise villains, signature heroes)
  - `VISUAL_RECOGNITION` (Cinematic frames, iconic prop close-ups)
  - `COMPLETION` (Famous cinematic quote endings)
  - `CAREER_PATH` (Director filmographies, actor franchise progressions)
- **Usable Archetypes**:
  - `NAME_FRAGMENT` (Character/actor names)
  - `SEQUENCE` (Franchise release chronological order)
  - `CONNECTION` (Shared ensemble casts)
  - `ODD_ONE_OUT` (Outlier film from director/franchise)
- **Archetypes to Limit**:
  - Box office exact grosses or minute runtimes.
  - Background extras without narrative impact.
- **Content Dimensions to Rotate Through**:
  - Heroes & Villains, Directors & Actors, Iconic Props & Vehicles, Plot Climax Moments, Cinematic Universes & Franchises.
- **World-Specific Anti-Patterns**:
  - *Book vs Movie Confusion*: Always anchor canon to the movie universe unless explicitly specified.
