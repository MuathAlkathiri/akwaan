# Scope: Movies Mix

- `scopeId`: `movies.movies-mix`
- `worldId`: `movies`
- Boundary: Controlled incubation pool of globally recognizable movies and
  franchises across action, horror, comedy, sci-fi, thriller, adventure, and
  major standalone films — including major franchises that do not yet have
  dedicated scopes (e.g. Jurassic Park, Star Wars, The Matrix, The Lord of the
  Rings, James Bond, Terminator, Back to the Future, Jaws, Inception,
  The Godfather, Titanic, Avatar, Pirates of the Caribbean).
- Included: famous movies, characters, props, scenes, worlds, and concepts that
  are culturally recognizable.
- Excluded: niche deep-cut lore from a single film; anything requiring
  franchise-specific insider knowledge to answer; random trivia dumps.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Pattern exclusions: cross-film comparisons without a named film.
- Safety: each item must name or clearly imply its film/franchise in the
  prompt; avoid spoilers and late-film twists. Arabic names with verified
  romanized aliases.
