# Movies Mix Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Mixed film pool: each item must be answerable from one flagship fact about the
named film — the film itself is part of the answer space.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Action/adventure: Indiana Jones (Indiana Jones, the whip, the Ark), James
  Bond (Bond, the Aston Martin, M), Terminator (the T-800, "I'll be back").
- Sci-fi: Star Wars (Luke, Vader, the Death Star, lightsabers), The Matrix
  (Neo, the red pill, Zion), Avatar (Pandora, the Na'vi, the Tree of Souls),
  Back to the Future (Marty, Doc, the DeLorean, 88 mph), Inception (the totem,
  dream levels).
- Thriller/horror: Jaws (the shark, Amity), The Silence of the Lambs
  (Hannibal Lecter, Clarice), Alien (the xenomorph, Ripley, the Nostromo).
- Drama/classic: The Godfather (Don Vito Corleone, the horse's head, the
  wedding), Titanic (the ship, the iceberg, Jack and Rose), Forrest Gump.
- Comedy: Home Alone (Kevin, the McCallisters, the traps), Ghostbusters (the
  ghosts, the proton packs, "Who you gonna call?").
- Epic fantasy: The Lord of the Rings (the One Ring, Frodo, Gandalf, Mordor),
  Pirates of the Caribbean (Jack Sparrow, the Black Pearl, Davy Jones).

## Stable Classifications

Character-to-film, prop-to-film, and quote-to-film associations are
deterministic and safe when the film is named or clearly implied.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Cross-franchise confusion, spoilers, and quote attribution are high risk.
Keep franchise diversity broad; do not let one franchise dominate a pool.
Prefer official film material. Record a spoiler endpoint with every unstable
claim.
