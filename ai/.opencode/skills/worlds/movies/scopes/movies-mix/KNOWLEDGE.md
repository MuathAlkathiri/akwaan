## Stable Classifications

Character-to-film, prop-to-film, and quote-to-film associations are
deterministic and safe when the film is named or clearly implied.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Cross-franchise confusion, spoilers, and quote attribution are high risk.
Keep franchise diversity broad; do not let one franchise dominate a pool.
Prefer official film material. Record a spoiler endpoint with every unstable
claim.
