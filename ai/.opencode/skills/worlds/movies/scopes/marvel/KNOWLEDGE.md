# Marvel Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Durable sets include the core Avengers, their powers and weapons, major
villains and organizations, the Infinity Saga, and signature locations.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Heroes (Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Hulk, Black Widow, Hawkeye,
  Spider-Man, Black Panther, Doctor Strange, Captain Marvel, the Guardians)
  and their stable powers.
- Villains and organizations (Thanos, Loki, Ultron, Killmonger, the Red Skull,
  HYDRA, S.H.I.E.L.D., the Ten Rings).
- Powers and weapons (Mjølnir, Stormbreaker, the Iron Man suits, Captain
  America's shield, the Infinity Stones and the Infinity Gauntlet).
- Locations (Asgard, Wakanda, Titan, the Sanctum, the Avengers compound, the
  Quantum Realm).
- Major events (the Snap, the Battle of New York, the Battle of Wakanda, the
  Endgame time-heist, the blip).

## Stable Classifications

Hero-to-power, villain-to-organization, and stone-to-event associations are
deterministic and safe when tied to the MCU.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Comics/MCU divergence, character power scaling, and late-Saga reveals are high
risk. Prefer MCU films as primary; record a spoiler endpoint with every
unstable claim. Arabic fandom spellings must match established usage.
