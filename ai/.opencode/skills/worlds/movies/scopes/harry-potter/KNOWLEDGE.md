# Harry Potter Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Durable sets include the core trio, Hogwarts staff and houses, the wizarding
world's locations, signature spells and objects, and major saga events.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Core characters (Harry Potter, Hermione Granger, Ron Weasley, Dumbledore,
  Snape, Voldemort, Hagrid, Draco Malfoy) and their stable roles.
- Hogwarts locations (Great Hall, Gryffindor common room, the Room of
  Requirement, Platform 9¾, Diagon Alley, Hogwarts Express, the Chamber of
  Secrets, Gringotts).
- Hogwarts houses (Gryffindor, Slytherin, Ravenclaw, Hufflepuff) with their
  stable founders and traits.
- Signature spells and objects (Expelliarmus, Expecto Patronum, the
  Elder Wand, the Invisibility Cloak, the Sorting Hat, the Golden Snitch,
  the Philosopher's Stone, Horcruxes).
- Creatures (Hedwig, Buckbeak, the Basilisk, Dobby, the Thestrals).

## Stable Classifications

Character-to-house, character-to-role, and object-to-event associations are
deterministic and safe when tied to the films.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Book/film divergences, spell-effect details, and late-saga reveals are high
risk. Prefer the films as primary; record a spoiler endpoint with every
unstable claim. Arabic fandom spellings must match established usage
(e.g. "هاري بوتر" not "هاري بوتر").
