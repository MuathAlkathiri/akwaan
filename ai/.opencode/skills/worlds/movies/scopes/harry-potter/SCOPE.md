# Scope: Harry Potter

- `scopeId`: `movies.harry-potter`
- `worldId`: `movies`
- Boundary: The eight Harry Potter films (Philosopher's Stone through Deathly
  Hallows Part 2) as the primary canon. Book-only material is included only
  where it is unambiguous and does not conflict with the films.
- Included: characters (students, teachers, Death Eaters, Order members),
  Hogwarts houses and locations, spells, magical objects, creatures, Horcruxes,
  major events, relationships, and recognizable scenes.
- Excluded: Fantastic Beasts films as a standalone answer space unless the fact
  is unambiguous within the main saga; ambiguous book/film divergence details.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Pattern exclusions: house-classification items that depend on book-only
  sorting criteria.
- Safety: avoid spoiling late-film outcomes in prompts and clues (e.g. who
  survives the Battle of Hogwarts, Snape's allegiance reveal). Arabic names
  with verified romanized aliases (هاري بوتر, هيرميون غرانجر, ألباس دمبلدور).
