# Disney & Pixar Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Durable sets include flagship films and franchises, their heroes and villains,
iconic locations, signature objects, and famous moments.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Heroes and protagonists (Simba, Elsa and Anna, Woody and Buzz, Nemo and
  Marlin, Aladdin, Moana, Mr. Incredible, Miguel, Lightning McQueen).
- Villains (Scar, Ursula, Jafar, Maleficent, Syndrome, Gaston, Mother
  Gothel, Sid).
- Films and franchises (The Lion King, Frozen, Toy Story, Finding Nemo,
  The Little Mermaid, Aladdin, The Incredibles, Moana, Coco, Up, Monsters
  Inc.).
- Locations and objects (Pride Lands, Arendelle, Andy's Room, the Nautilus
  free swim, the Magic Carpet, the Genie's lamp, Radiator Springs, the red
  balloon).
- Songs as context (Circle of Life, Let It Go, Under the Sea, A Whole New
  World, Hakuna Matata).

## Stable Classifications

Character-to-film, villain-to-film, and object-to-film associations are
deterministic and safe.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Franchise cross-overs and song-lyric details are high risk. Keep answer
families spread across franchises so no single film dominates a pool. Prefer
the animated features as primary. Arabic fandom spellings must match
established usage.
