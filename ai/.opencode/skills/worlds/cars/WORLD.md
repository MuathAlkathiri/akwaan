# World: السيارات (Cars)

- `worldId`: `cars`
- Identity: car recognition, brands, models, automotive culture, performance,
  engineering identity, and enthusiast knowledge.
- Tone: enthusiast-friendly, recognition-led, discussion-friendly. People who
  enjoy cars — not professional automotive engineers.
- Media anchors: silhouettes, headlights, taillights, front grilles,
  dashboards, interiors, wheel designs, badges, generations, side profiles,
  engine sounds, exhaust notes (future media pass — not a completion blocker).
- Safety: no VIN/spec-sheet memorization, no random part numbers, no meaningless
  production statistics, no obscure trim trivia, no arbitrary option-package
  knowledge. Visual-heavy identity.
- Time-sensitive: current model-year/price facts require up-to-date verification
  and should be avoided in favor of stable model/generation identity.
- Signature mechanic: unassigned; the World is not launch-ready. Keep the World
  and Scopes draft; do not activate or configure boards.

This World supplies presentation context only.
