# Supercars Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Durable sets include supercar makers, iconic models, engines, and design
identity.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Manufacturers: Ferrari, Lamborghini, McLaren, Bugatti, Pagani, Koenigsegg.
- Iconic models: Ferrari F40, LaFerrari, 488, F8, SF90; Lamborghini Aventador,
  Huracán, Countach, Miura; McLaren P1, F1, 720S; Bugatti Veyron, Chiron;
  Pagani Zonda, Huayra; Koenigsegg Agera, Jesko, CCX.
- Famous tech: gullwing/scissor doors, active aero, hybrid hypercar systems.
- Production: limited-run figures where reliably established.

## Stable Classifications

Model-to-manufacturer, model-to-engine, and model-to-era associations are
deterministic and safe.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Top-speed claims, unofficial performance figures, and volatile prices are high
risk — avoid unless historically fixed and precisely contextualized. Define
exact variant for power/displacement. Arabic terms must match established usage.
