# Cars Mix Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Durable sets include recognizable global models and automotive icons across
cultures.

## Safe Entity Sets

- American: Ford Mustang, Mustang GT, Chevrolet Camaro, Corvette, Dodge
  Challenger/Charger, Hellcat.
- British: Aston Martin DB5, DB11, Bentley Continental GT, Rolls-Royce Phantom,
  Mini Cooper, Range Rover.
- Off-road/pickup: Jeep Wrangler, Land Rover Defender, Ford F-150, Toyota Hilux
  (as icon), RAM.
- Electric: Tesla Model S, Model 3, Roadster, Rivian.
- Icons: VW Beetle (already German — avoid overlap), Mini, Land Rover Series.

## Stable Classifications

Model-to-manufacturer and model-to-country-of-origin associations are
deterministic and safe.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Cross-scope overlap and exact-variant specs are high risk. Prefer stable
identity facts. Arabic terms must match established usage.
