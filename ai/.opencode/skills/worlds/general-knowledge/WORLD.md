# World: معلومات عامة (General Knowledge)

- `worldId`: `general-knowledge`
- Identity: interesting knowledge people enjoy knowing and discussing —
  science, history, inventions & discoveries, and the human body & nature.
- Tone: "أوه حلوة المعلومة!" / "أكيد أعرف هذي!" — entertaining, recognition-led,
  discussion-friendly. NOT a school exam.
- Media anchors: diagrams, scientific imagery, historical images, invention
  images, animals/nature, anatomy diagrams (future media pass — not a
  completion blocker).
- Safety: no obscure textbook terminology, no arbitrary dates, no overly
  academic wording, no highly technical equations, no trivia requiring
  specialist education, no random memorization, no deceptive wording.
- Boundaries: do NOT pull in world-geography content (العالم) or Saudi-specific
  content (السعودية). Not a second geography world.
- Signature mechanic: unassigned; the World is not launch-ready. Keep the World
  and Scopes draft; do not activate or configure boards.

This World supplies presentation context only.

## Question Palette

- **High-Value Archetypes**:
  - `FAST_ATTRIBUTE` (Chemical symbols, planetary orders, inventor nationalities)
  - `CATEGORY_IDENTIFICATION` (Triangulate historical era, scientific phenomenon, or organ function)
  - `DETAIL_RECOGNITION` (Anatomical systems, fundamental scientific laws, iconic inventions)
  - `REVERSE_QUESTION` (Invention -> Inventor, Theory -> Discoverer)
  - `BEFORE_AFTER` (Historical milestones, technological revolutions)
- **Usable Archetypes**:
  - `NAME_FRAGMENT` (Famous scientists and inventors)
  - `ODD_ONE_OUT` (Periodic table groups, taxonomy classifications)
  - `SEQUENCE` (Planetary distances, historical dynastic sequences)
- **Archetypes to Limit**:
  - Arbitrary multi-digit constants (e.g. speed of light to 5 decimal places).
- **Content Dimensions to Rotate Through**:
  - Space & Astronomy, Human Body & Medicine, Physics & Chemistry, World Inventions & Discoveries, Landmark World History.
- **World-Specific Anti-Patterns**:
  - *Academic Textbook Jargon*: Keep questions conversational and relatable to real-world phenomena.
