# History Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Durable sets include civilizations, empires, rulers, wars, explorers, and major
events across regions.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Civilizations: Ancient Egypt, Roman Empire, Ancient Greece, Persia,
  Han China, Islamic Golden Age, Inca, Maya.
- Rulers/leaders: Julius Caesar, Cleopatra, Genghis Khan, Alexander the Great,
  Napoleon, Queen Victoria.
- Events: French Revolution, American Revolution, World War I, World War II,
  first Moon landing (1969).
- Explorers: Christopher Columbus, Marco Polo, Ibn Battuta, Vasco da Gama.

## Stable Classifications

Civilization-to-location, leader-to-event, and event-to-century associations
are deterministic and safe for well-established facts.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Disputed dates/territorial claims, uncertain ancient figures, and contested
attribution are high risk. Verify before authoring; choose a different fact if
sources disagree. Arabic terms must match established usage.
