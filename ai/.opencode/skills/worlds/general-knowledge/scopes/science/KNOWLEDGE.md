# Science Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Durable sets include planets, scientists, elements, phenomena, and recognizable
science concepts.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Planets: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune.
- Scientists: Albert Einstein, Isaac Newton, Marie Curie, Galileo, Charles
  Darwin, Nikola Tesla.
- Elements/common: oxygen, water (H2O), carbon dioxide, helium, gold, iron.
- Phenomena: gravity, lightning, sound speed, light speed, evaporation,
  condensation, photosynthesis.

## Stable Classifications

Planet-to-property, scientist-to-discovery, and phenomenon-to-explanation
associations are deterministic and safe.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Numeric precision (planet diameters, distances) should be estimable, not
8-decimal-point trivia. Use sensible units and tolerance. Arabic terms must
match established usage.
