# Inventions & Discoveries Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Durable sets include inventions, inventors, discoveries, and technology
progression.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Inventions: light bulb, telephone, printing press, airplane, automobile,
  penicillin, television, computer, internet, photography, radio.
- Inventors (well-attributed): Edison (light bulb commercialization), Bell
  (telephone patent), Gutenberg (printing press), the Wright Brothers
  (first powered flight), Fleming (penicillin discovery).
- Discoveries: penicillin, DNA structure (Watson & Crick), gravity (Newton),
  relativity (Einstein).

## Stable Classifications

Invention-to-purpose, invention-to-era, and discovery-to-scientist
associations are deterministic and safe when attribution is well-established.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Contested invention attribution is high risk — use precise framing (inventor vs
patent holder vs commercializer). Verify before authoring. Arabic terms must
match established usage.
