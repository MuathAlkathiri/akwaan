# Human Body & Nature Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Durable sets include organs, bones, animals, plants, and natural phenomena.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Human body: heart, lungs, brain, liver, skin (largest organ), bones (206 in
  adult), blood, stomach, senses.
- Animals: elephant, cheetah (fastest land animal), blue whale (largest animal),
  giraffe, octopus, penguin, kangaroo, bee.
- Plants: tree, cactus, sunflower, rose.
- Nature: photosynthesis, migration, ocean, rainforest, desert, mountain.

## Stable Classifications

Organ-to-function, animal-to-ability, and species-to-habitat associations are
deterministic and safe for well-established facts.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Biological variation and viral animal myths are high risk. Use stable,
well-established quantities; frame approximate values as approximate. Arabic
terms must match established usage.
