# Scope: Human Body & Nature

- `scopeId`: `general-knowledge.human-body-nature`
- `worldId`: `general-knowledge`
- Boundary: accessible human body, animals, plants, ecosystems, nature, and
  biological phenomena — balanced, not all human body.
- Included: organs, bones, senses, blood, heart, lungs, brain, digestion, body
  systems; animals and their abilities/habitats; plants; forests, oceans,
  ecosystems, migration, extreme species, natural cycles, adaptation.
- Excluded: medical diagnosis, treatment advice, pathological edge cases,
  clinical terminology; viral "animal facts" that are myths.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Pattern exclusions: none.
- Safety: use well-established facts; avoid viral myths; don't phrase variable
  biological values as exact universal constants.
