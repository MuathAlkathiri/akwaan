# Resident Evil Knowledge Base (قاعدة معرفة Resident Evil)

## Scope Summary
Capcom's pioneering survival horror franchise. Focuses on iconic bio-weapons, memorable protagonists, terrifying stalker villains, pharmaceutical conspiracies (Umbrella), inventory management, and key outbreak incidents.

## Key Territories & Recognizable Entities
- **Iconic Protagonists:**
  - Leon S. Kennedy (Rookie RPD cop in RE2, secret agent in Spain saving President's daughter Ashley Graham in RE4).
  - Chris Redfield (S.T.A.R.S. Alpha Team marksman, BSAA founder, boulder puncher in RE5).
  - Jill Valentine (S.T.A.R.S. master of unlocking in RE1, hunted by Nemesis across Raccoon City in RE3).
  - Claire Redfield (Chris's sister searching for him in RE2 and Code: Veronica, protects Sherry Birkin).
  - Ethan Winters (Faceless everyman saving Mia in RE7 Baker plantation and daughter Rose in RE8 Village).
  - Ada Wong (Mysterious red-dress spy working for enigmatic organizations).
- **Iconic Villains, Tyrants & Bosses:**
  - Albert Wesker (S.T.A.R.S. traitor, sunglasses, injected with virus, superhuman speed & strength).
  - Nemesis (STARS-hunting bio-weapon with rocket launcher and tentacles in RE3).
  - Mr. X / Tyrant T-00 (Fedora-wearing unstoppable stalker in RPD in RE2).
  - Osmund Saddler & Ramon Salazar (Los Iluminados cult leaders in RE4).
  - Lady Alcina Dimitrescu (Giant 9-foot vampire countess in Castle Dimitrescu in RE8).
  - Jack Baker ("Welcome to the family, son!" in RE7).
  - William Birkin (G-Virus creator mutated with giant eyeball on his shoulder).
- **Viruses, Parasites & Corporations:**
  - Umbrella Corporation (شركة أمبريلا / Red and white umbrella logo, "Our business is life itself").
  - S.T.A.R.S. (Special Tactics and Rescue Service in Raccoon City).
  - Viruses: T-Virus (Tyrant virus causing zombie outbreak), G-Virus, Las Plagas (parasite in Spain), The Mold / Megamycete.
- **Iconic Items & Mechanics:**
  - Green Herb (عشبة خضراء - healing), Red Herb (combines to boost healing), Yellow Herb (boosts max health in RE4).
  - Typewriter (آلة كاتبة) and Ink Ribbons (أشرطة الحبر - saving game).
  - Merchant ("What're ya buyin', stranger?" in RE4).
  - Raccoon City destruction by nuclear missile strike (October 1, 1998).
