### Legacy Taxonomy Data:
# FIFA Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Key sets include titles in the FIFA series (FIFA International Soccer 1993
through FIFA 23, renamed EA Sports FC from 2023), editions, gameplay modes
(Ultimate Team, Career Mode, VOLTA Football, Kick-Off, online modes), cover
athletes, licensed leagues, clubs, stadiums, and the football rules embodied
in the game.

## Stable Classifications

Edition names, the 1993 first-release year, cover athletes per edition,
gameplay-mode names, and core football rules (90-minute standard match, 22
players, offside) are deterministic and can be treated as stable. Live player
ratings, FUT market values, and chemistry are unstable and require dated
sources.

## Recognition Saturation

Ultimate Team, Career Mode, and iconic cover athletes (Kylian Mbappé, Lionel
Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo) are instantly recognized by many players. Use them as
context or comparison rather than relying on bare identification.

## Media Anchors

Cover art, kit and crest designs, stadiums, player-card art, clean UI
elements, and bounded gameplay clips. Avoid menus or audio that state the
intended result.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Ultimate Team content and live-data values (player ratings, prices, chemistry,
live transfers) change frequently and require a version and date label.
Translation and regional cover variants differ; prefer official EA material and
reliable gameplay evidence when needed.
