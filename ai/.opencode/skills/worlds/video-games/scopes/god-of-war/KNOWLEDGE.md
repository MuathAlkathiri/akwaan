# God of War Knowledge Base (قاعدة معرفة God of War)

## Scope Summary
Sony PlayStation's flagship mythic action-adventure franchise by Santa Monica Studio following the tragic and brutal journey of Kratos from Sparta to Olympus and the Norse Nine Realms.

## Key Territories & Recognizable Entities
- **Core Protagonists & Companions:**
  - Kratos (Ghost of Sparta / شبح سبارتا, tattooed with ashes of his family, Greek God of War).
  - Atreus / Loki (Kratos's son, archer, shapeshifter, giant / Jötunn heritage).
  - Mimir (Smartest Man Alive / أحكم رجل على قيد الحياة, severed talking head attached to Kratos's belt).
  - Freya (Vanir goddess, former Queen of the Valkyries, mother of Baldur, Witch of the Woods).
  - Brok and Sindri (The Huldra Brothers / dwarven blacksmiths who forged Leviathan Axe and Mjölnir).
- **Greek Era Gods & Foes:**
  - Zeus (King of Olympus, father of Kratos, master of lightning).
  - Ares (Original God of War who tricked Kratos into killing his family).
  - Athena (Goddess of wisdom who guides Kratos in spirit).
  - Poseidon (God of the sea, opening boss of GoW 3), Hades (Lord of the Underworld), Helios (Sun god whose head lights dark places), Hermes (Messenger god whose boots give super speed), Hercules, Kronos (Titan carrying Pandora's Temple).
- **Norse Era Gods & Bosses:**
  - Odin (The All-Father, ruler of Asgard, manipulative arch-villain).
  - Thor (God of Thunder, wielder of Mjölnir / مطرقة ميولنير).
  - Baldur (Invulnerable son of Freya, blessed with invulnerability to all threats physical or magical except mistletoe).
  - Heimdall (Watcher of the Aesir, reads minds, rides Gulltoppr).
  - Sigrun (Queen of the Valkyries, infamous super-boss).
- **Legendary Weapons & Artifacts:**
  - Blades of Chaos (سيوف الفوضى - dual chained blades fused to Kratos's arms by Ares).
  - Leviathan Axe (فأس ليفياثان - frost axe forged by Brok & Sindri for Laufey / Faye).
  - Draupnir Spear (رمح دروبنير - wind/duplicating spear forged in Ragnarök with the lady of the forge).
  - Blade of Olympus (سيف أوليمبوس - giant glowing blue sword forged by Zeus).
  - Mjölnir (hammer of Thor).
  - Guardian Shield (درع الحارس).
- **The Nine Realms (العوالم التسعة):** Midgard (Earth/home), Alfheim (Light & Dark Elves), Helheim (Realm of the dead), Jotunheim (Realm of the Giants), Svartalfheim (Dwarven realm), Vanaheim (Vanir gods), Niflheim (Mist/fog), Muspelheim (Fire/trials of Surtr), Asgard (Home of Odin).

## Ambiguity Traps & Transliteration
- Kratos's title: شبح سبارتا (Ghost of Sparta).
- Weapons: سيوف الفوضى (Blades of Chaos), فأس ليفياثان (Leviathan Axe), رمح دروبنير (Draupnir Spear).
