### Legacy Taxonomy Data:
# GTA Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Key sets include mainline titles (III, Vice City, San Andreas, IV, V, VI),
protagonists, cities and states, gangs, radio stations, vehicles, missions, and
maps within the mainline continuity.

## Stable Classifications

City of origin, state, protagonist, title of origin, and feature introduction
are deterministic when the universe (3D vs. HD) and edition are explicit. Sales
and live figures are dated and require the reporting period.

## Recognition Saturation

Los Santos, Michael, Franklin, Trevor, Niko Bellic, Carl Johnson, Tommy
Vercetti, Liberty City, and Vice City are instantly recognized by many casual
players. Use them as context or comparison rather than relying on bare
identification.

## Media Anchors

Distinctive city skylines, character silhouettes, vehicle lineups, radio-branded
sound cues, and bounded gameplay clips. Avoid menus or audio that state the
intended result.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Cities and states recur across universes with different layouts, and names
carry translated-name risks (Arabic/localized spellings such as «لوس سانتوس» and
«فايس سيتي»). New releases require spoiler-safe wording and dated labels.
Prefer official Rockstar Games and Take-Two material, supported by reliable
franchise wikis when needed.
