# Minecraft Knowledge Base (قاعدة معرفة ماينكرافت)

## Scope Summary
The world's best-selling sandbox video game by Mojang. Content focuses on durable, universally recognized mechanics, iconic mobs, dimensions, crafting, ores, and exploration structures.

## Key Territories & Recognizable Entities
- **Iconic Mobs:**
  - Hostile: Creeper (hissing explosion, drops gunpowder), Enderman (teleportation, picks up blocks, hostile if looked in the eyes), Zombie, Skeleton (bow and arrow), Spider, Witch, Slime, Phantom, Drowned, Pillager / Vindicator / Evoker (Raids).
  - Nether Mobs: Ghast (flying fireball shooter), Blaze (spins fire rods in fortress), Piglin (barters for gold), Wither Skeleton (inflicts Wither effect, drops skulls), Magma Cube, Hoglin, Strider (walks on lava).
  - Deep Dark: The Warden (blind, detects vibrations, sonic boom attack, spawned by Sculk Shrieker).
  - Passive & Utility: Villager (trades emeralds), Iron Golem (protects villages, built with 4 iron blocks + carved pumpkin), Snow Golem, Wolf, Cat, Horse, Axolotl, Bee.
- **Boss Mobs:**
  - Ender Dragon (in The End dimension, healed by End Crystals atop obsidian pillars, drops dragon egg).
  - The Wither (summoned with 4 Soul Sand in T-shape + 3 Wither Skeleton Skulls, drops Nether Star for Beacons).
  - Elder Guardian (Ocean Monument, inflicts Mining Fatigue).
- **Dimensions & Exploration Structures:**
  - Overworld: Stronghold (End Portal frame requiring 12 Eyes of Ender), Village, Desert Temple (TNT trap beneath pressure plate), Jungle Temple, Mineshaft, Woodland Mansion (Totem of Undying), Ocean Monument, Ancient City (Deep Dark).
  - The Nether: Nether Portal (Obsidian frame lit with Flint and Steel), Nether Fortress (Blazes, Nether Wart), Bastion Remnant.
  - The End: End Portal, End Cities (Chorus Fruit, Shulkers, End Ship with Elytra wings).
- **Crafting, Ores & Equipment:**
  - Ores: Netherite (strongest material made from Ancient Debris in the Nether), Diamond, Gold, Iron, Redstone, Emerald, Lapis Lazuli (enchanting).
  - Tools & Weapons: Pickaxe, Sword, Axe, Bow, Crossbow, Trident, Shield, Elytra (wings for gliding), Totem of Undying (prevents death).
  - Utility Blocks: Crafting Table (3x3 grid), Furnace / Blast Furnace / Smoker, Anvil, Enchantment Table (surrounded by 15 bookshelves), Brewing Stand, Beacon.

## Ambiguity Traps & Normalization
- The strongest material: Netherite (نيثرايت / نيزيرايت) replaced Diamond in Update 1.16.
- Nether Star is dropped by The Wither (الويذر) and used to craft a Beacon (منارة / بيكون).
- Elytra (اليترا / أجنحة الإليترا) found in End Ships.
