### Legacy Taxonomy Data:
# Call of Duty Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Key sets include titles, eras, multiplayer maps, modes, objectives, weapons,
attachments, equipment, perks, scorestreaks, killstreaks, operators, factions,
campaign missions, Zombies locations, machines, and sound cues.

## Stable Classifications

Weapon class, engagement range, map environment, game mode, equipment function,
title of origin, and playable status can be deterministic when edition and patch
are explicit. Live balance values are unstable and require dated sources.

## Recognition Saturation

Rust, Nuketown, Shipment, Terminal, and other iconic elements are instantly
recognized by many casual players. Use them as context or comparison rather
than relying on bare identification.

## Media Anchors

Distinctive map areas, weapon silhouettes, clean UI elements, equipment,
announcer-free sound effects, and bounded gameplay clips. Avoid menus or audio
that state the intended result.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Remakes, reused names, mode variants, seasonal changes, and platform differences
require title, edition, mode, and date labels. Prefer official game data and
publisher material, supported by reliable gameplay evidence when needed.
