# Overwatch Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Key sets include playable heroes (e.g. Reinhardt, Tracer, Mercy, Genji, Zarya),
the three roles (tank, damage, support), abilities, ultimates, maps, gameplay
modes, esports leagues, and lore factions (Overwatch, Talon, Shimada Clan,
Null Sector). Series context: Overwatch (2016) and Overwatch 2 (2022).

## Stable Classifications

Hero-to-role assignment (tank/damage/support), hero origin country, map game
mode, and the ability/ultimate-to-hero mapping are deterministic across
versions and can be treated as stable. Roster counts are only stable when a
release or season date is attached.

## Recognition Saturation

Reinhardt, Tracer, Mercy, Genji, and other launch-era icons are instantly
recognized by many casual players. Use them as context or comparison rather
than relying on bare identification.

## Media Anchors

Distinctive hero silhouettes, ability and ultimate effects, iconic map areas,
clean UI elements, and bounded gameplay clips. Avoid menus or audio that state
the intended result.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Translation differs across regions (hero and ability names are localized).
Balance-patch changelogs and seasonal roster changes (new heroes, hero pools,
role changes) require a version and date label. Prefer official Blizzard game
data and publisher material, supported by reliable gameplay evidence when
needed.
