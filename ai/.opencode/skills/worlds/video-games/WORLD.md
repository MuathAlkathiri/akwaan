# World: Video Games

- `worldId`: `video-games`
- Identity: player action, maps, interfaces, equipment, modes, sounds,
  characters, objectives, and memorable outcomes.
- Tone: kinetic, competitive, tactile, and recognition-led.
- Media anchors: authentic gameplay, UI crops, maps, sound effects, equipment,
  and bounded clips.
- Safety: distinguish editions, platforms, modes, and live-service versions.
- Presentation: controller-inspired input framing, game-specific sound profile,
  and fast reveal motion.
- Signature mechanic: unassigned; the World is not launch-ready.

This World supplies presentation context only.

## Question Palette

- **High-Value Archetypes**:
  - `DETAIL_RECOGNITION` (Wonder weapons, killstreaks, map locations, iconic UI elements)
  - `CHARACTER_TO_WORK` & `WORK_TO_CHARACTER` (Boss <-> Game, Hero <-> Franchise)
  - `AUDIO_RECOGNITION` (Level SFX, tactical nuke alarms, iconic character voice cues)
  - `NAME_FRAGMENT` & `COMPLETE_THE_NAME` (Character first/last names, studio names)
  - `VISUAL_RECOGNITION` & `PARTIAL_VISUAL` (Clean authentic gameplay frames, weapon silhouettes)
  - `FAST_ATTRIBUTE` (Hero class/role, console generation, primary element/weapon)
- **Usable Archetypes**:
  - `REAL_NAME` (Hero alter-egos: `"Symmetra" -> Satya Vaswani`)
  - `SEQUENCE` (Franchise release chronology)
  - `NICKNAME_OR_ALIAS` (Faction monikers, boss titles: `"Gwyn" -> Lord of Cinder`)
  - `BEFORE_AFTER` (Hardware/game release timing)
- **Archetypes to Limit**:
  - Obscure patch balance numbers (e.g. `"كم زاد الضرر في التحديث 1.04؟"`).
  - Mainstream bosses labeled as Hard (Bosses like Nemesis, Warden, Tenpenny are Medium/Easy).
- **Content Dimensions to Rotate Through**:
  - Playable Heroes, Villains & Bosses, Maps & Arenas, Weapons & Artifacts, Factions & Guilds, UI & Gameplay Mechanics, Lore & Campaign Events.
- **World-Specific Anti-Patterns**:
  - *Patch Fragility*: Never author items dependent on temporary meta stats, live multipliers, or fleeting server patches.
