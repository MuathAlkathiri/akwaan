# World: Anime

- `worldId`: `anime`
- Identity: stories, emotion, visual recognition, fandom memory, powers, places,
  factions, turning points, voices, and iconic sequences.
- Tone: energetic, nostalgic, dramatic, playful, spoiler-aware.
- Media anchors: canonical frames, bounded clips, clean dialogue, openings,
  symbols, maps, and diagrams.
- Safety: enforce declared adaptation and spoiler boundaries.
- Presentation: distinctive animation-inspired frame, energetic sound profile,
  and concise Arabic fandom vocabulary.
- Signature mechanic: unassigned; this World is not launch-ready until one
  exclusive auto-resolvable mechanic is approved.

This file supplies presentation context only. It does not own mechanic behavior
or Content Patterns.

## Question Palette

- **High-Value Archetypes**:
  - `VISUAL_RECOGNITION` & `PARTIAL_VISUAL` (Iconic eye forms, cursed objects, weapon frames)
  - `NAME_FRAGMENT` & `COMPLETE_THE_NAME` (Family names, spell incantations, jutsu names)
  - `NICKNAME_OR_ALIAS` (Epithets: `"وميض كونوها الأصفر" -> ميناتو`)
  - `CHARACTER_TO_WORK` & `WORK_TO_CHARACTER` (Characters, anime studios, flagship arcs)
  - `WHO_SAID_OR_DID_IT` (Unforgettable meme quotes and historic turning-point acts)
  - `FAST_ATTRIBUTE` (Bounty tier, squad/division number, cursed grade)
- **Usable Archetypes**:
  - `COMPLETION` (Attack names, crew oaths)
  - `CONNECTION` (Shared crew, mentor-student bonds)
  - `DETAIL_RECOGNITION` (Zanpakuto names, Devil Fruit powers, Titan forms)
- **Archetypes to Limit**:
  - Arc episode numbers (e.g. `"في أي حلقة ظهر فلان؟"`).
  - Disputed filler canon vs manga canon unless explicitly scoped.
- **Content Dimensions to Rotate Through**:
  - Main Protagonists, Antagonists & Organizations, Signature Abilities & Spells, Relics & Weapons, Legendary Battles, Factions & Squads.
- **World-Specific Anti-Patterns**:
  - *Spoiler Leaks*: Avoid late-manga unadapted plot twists.
