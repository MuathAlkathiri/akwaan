# Demon Slayer Knowledge Base (قاعدة معرفة قاتل الشياطين)

## Scope Summary
The global phenomenon animated by Ufotable. Content focuses on the Demon Slayer Corps, breathing techniques, the 9 Hashira, Demon King Muzan, the Twelve Kizuki, and Nichirin swords.

## Key Territories & Recognizable Entities
- **Main Characters:** Tanjiro Kamado (Sun/Water Breathing, black Nichirin sword, hanafuda earrings), Nezuko Kamado (demon sister with bamboo muzzle, Blood Demon Art: Exploding Blood), Zenitsu Agatsuma (Thunder Breathing: First Form Thunderclap and Flash), Inosuke Hashibira (Beast Breathing, boar mask, serrated dual swords), Kanao Tsuyuri, Genya Shinazugawa.
- **The 9 Hashira (Pillars / الهاشيرا):**
  1. Giyu Tomioka (Water Hashira - هاشيرا الماء)
  2. Kyojuro Rengoku (Flame Hashira - هاشيرا اللهب - Set your heart ablaze)
  3. Shinobu Kocho (Insect Hashira - هاشيرا الحشرات - Wisteria poison)
  4. Tengen Uzui (Sound Hashira - هاشيرا الصوت - God of Flashiness)
  5. Muichiro Tokito (Mist Hashira - هاشيرا الضباب)
  6. Mitsuri Kanroji (Love Hashira - هاشيرا الحب - flexible whip sword)
  7. Sanemi Shinazugawa (Wind Hashira - هاشيرا الرياح - scarred brother of Genya)
  8. Gyomei Himejima (Stone Hashira - هاشيرا الصخر - blind monk, flail & axe)
  9. Obanai Iguro (Serpent Hashira - هاشيرا الأفعى - snake Kaburamaru).
- **Antagonists (Muzan & Twelve Kizuki / الأقمار الاثنا عشر):** Muzan Kibutsuji (Demon Progenitor), Upper Rank 1 (Kokushibo - Moon Breathing), Upper Rank 2 (Doma - Ice), Upper Rank 3 (Akaza - Destructive Death martial artist), Upper Rank 4 (Hantengu / Nakime), Upper Rank 5 (Gyokko), Upper Rank 6 (Gyutaro & Daki), Lower Rank 1 (Enmu - Mugen Train), Lower Rank 5 (Rui - Spider family).
- **Breathing Styles (أنفاس القتال):** Sun Breathing (Hinokami Kagura / رقصة إله النار), Water, Flame, Thunder, Beast, Mist, Love, Wind, Stone, Sound, Insect, Moon.
- **Key Concepts & Artifacts:** Nichirin Blade (سيف النيتشيرين / changes color), Wisteria Flowers (أزهار الويستيريا - repels demons), Demon Slayer Mark (علامة قاتل الشياطين), Final Selection on Mt. Fujikasane, Swordsmith Village (Hotaru Haganezuka).

## Ambiguity Traps & Transliteration
- Tanjiro's breathing: Hinokami Kagura (هينوكامي كاغورا) is Sun Breathing (تنفس الشمس).
- Hashira titles in Arabic: هاشيرا الماء، هاشيرا اللهب، هاشيرا الصوت، إلخ.
