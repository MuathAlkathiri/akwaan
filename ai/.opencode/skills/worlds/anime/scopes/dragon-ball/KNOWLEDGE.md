# Dragon Ball Knowledge Base (قاعدة معرفة دراغون بول)

## Scope Summary
The quintessential martial arts Shonen franchise created by Akira Toriyama. Content covers legendary fights, power transformations, alien races, signature techniques, and magical artifacts.

## Key Territories & Recognizable Entities
- **Core Protagonists & Z-Fighters:** Son Goku (Kakarot), Vegeta (Prince of Saiyans), Son Gohan, Piccolo (Namekian warrior), Future Trunks, Krillin, Yamcha, Tien Shinhan, Master Roshi (Kame-Sennin), Android 17 & 18.
- **Major Villains & Antagonists:** King Piccolo, Raditz, Nappa, Frieza (and Ginyu Force), Androids (16, 19, 20/Gero), Cell (Perfect Cell), Majin Buu (Fat Buu, Super Buu, Kid Buu), Babidi, Beerus (God of Destruction), Golden Frieza, Goku Black / Zamasu, Jiren, Broly.
- **Transformations & Power Stages:** Super Saiyan (SSJ1, SSJ2, SSJ3), Super Saiyan God (Red), Super Saiyan Blue (SSGSS), Ultra Instinct (Sign & Mastered), Ultra Ego, Super Saiyan Rosé, Beast Gohan, Orange Piccolo, Golden / Black Frieza.
- **Signature Techniques & Ki Attacks:** Kamehameha, Genki Dama (Spirit Bomb), Galick Gun, Final Flash, Big Bang Attack, Makankosappo (Special Beam Cannon), Kienzan (Destructo Disc), Solar Flare (Taiyoken), Kaioken, Instant Transmission (Shunkan Ido), Fusion Dance (Gogeta) / Potara Earrings (Vegito).
- **Artifacts, Objects & Locations:** 7 Dragon Balls, Shenron / Porunga / Super Shenron, Senzu Beans, Dragon Radar, Power Scouter, Gravity Chamber, Hyperbolic Time Chamber (Room of Spirit and Time), Planet Namek, Planet Vegeta, King Kai's Planet, World Martial Arts Tournament (Tenkaichi Budokai), Universe 7 & Tournament of Power arena.

## Ambiguity Traps & Normalization
- Distinguish between Potara fusion (Vegito / فيجيتو) and Metamoran dance fusion (Gogeta / غوجيتا).
- Note Arabic transliterations: غوكو / جوكو, فيجيتا / بيجيتا, غوهان / جوهان, كاميهاميها, كرات التنين.
