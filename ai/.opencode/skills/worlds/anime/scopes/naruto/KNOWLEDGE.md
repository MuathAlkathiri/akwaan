### Legacy Taxonomy Data:
# Naruto Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Key sets include shinobi villages, clans, teams, ranks, chakra natures, jutsu,
dōjutsu, summons, tailed beasts, organizations, weapons, locations, wars, and arcs.

## Safe Entity Sets

Use established characters beyond only the central protagonist, village and
clan symbols, team membership at named eras, recognizable abilities, tools,
locations, and consequential events.

## Stable Classifications

Village origin, clan, chakra nature, team assignment, organization membership,
ability class, and weapon type are usable when time-dependent changes are stated.

## Media Anchors

Village emblems, headbands, weapons, hand signs, distinctive environments,
ability effects, voices, and event frames. Avoid frames whose subtitles or HUD
labels expose the result.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Aliases, transliteration, filler continuity, temporary teams, reincarnation,
forms, and identity reveals need explicit boundaries. Prefer official episode
material and licensed guides; retain Arabic and romanized accepted forms.
