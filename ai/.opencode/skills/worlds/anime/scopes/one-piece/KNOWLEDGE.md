### Legacy Taxonomy Data:
# One Piece Knowledge

## Identity and Vocabulary

Core vocabulary includes pirates, Marines, crews, captains, islands, seas,
Grand Line, New World, Devil Fruits, Haki, bounties, ships, factions, titles,
and named arcs. Use established Arabic fandom spellings with verified English
or romanized aliases.

## Safe Entity Sets

- Straw Hat crew and other established crews within the spoiler boundary.
- Marines, Revolutionary Army, World Government institutions, and named factions.
- Islands, kingdoms, ships, weapons, symbols, abilities, and major events.

## Stable Relationships and Classifications

Crew membership, institutional affiliation, captain-to-crew association,
island-to-sea placement, ability type, and arc-era presence are useful only when
the selected era makes membership deterministic.

## Numeric Dimensions

Bounties, counts, sequence positions, and dates require an explicit canonical
snapshot because many change over time. Never use a live or unlabeled value.

## Media Anchors

Distinctive flags, ships, islands, uniforms, weapons, transformations, voices,
and bounded event frames. Remove subtitles, titles, watermarks, and visible names.

## Ambiguity and Sources

Continuity, translation, temporary alliances, and evolving titles are common
ambiguity risks. Prefer official anime material and licensed reference data;
record the spoiler endpoint and source date with every unstable claim.
