# Scope: Countries & Flags

- `scopeId`: `world.countries-flags`
- `worldId`: `world`
- Boundary: countries, national flags, capitals, currencies (where stable and
  useful), neighboring countries, continents, country shapes, national symbols,
  country recognition, and geographic position.
- Included: major recognizable countries, their flags, capitals, currencies,
  continents, and neighbors.
- Excluded: obscure flag-law trivia, minor administrative subdivisions,
  volatile political trivia, arbitrary economic rankings, current-population
  statistics.
- `excludedChallengeTypeIds`: []
- Pattern exclusions: invention/trivia items with a nationality.
- Safety: use stable geographic definitions; date-bound volatile facts.
