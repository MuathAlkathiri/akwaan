# Serie A Knowledge Base (قاعدة معرفة الدوري الإيطالي)

## Scope Summary
The premier men's football league in Italy, renowned for tactical mastery, passionate regional derbies, and the historic "Sette Sorelle" (Seven Sisters) golden era.

## Key Territories & Recognizable Entities
- **Iconic Clubs:** Juventus (The Old Lady / Bianconeri), AC Milan (Rossoneri), Inter Milan (Nerazzurri), AS Roma (Giallorossi), SS Lazio (Biancocelesti), SSC Napoli (Partenopei), ACF Fiorentina (Viola), Parma, Sampdoria, Torino, Atalanta.
- **Historic Derbies & Rivalries:** Derby della Madonnina (Milan vs Inter), Derby d'Italia (Juventus vs Inter), Derby della Capitale (Roma vs Lazio), Derby della Mole (Juventus vs Torino), Derby della Lanterna (Genoa vs Sampdoria).
- **Legendary Icons:** Paolo Maldini, Francesco Totti, Alessandro Del Piero, Javier Zanetti, Roberto Baggio, Andrea Pirlo, Gianluigi Buffon, Franco Baresi, Gabriel Batistuta, Marco van Basten, Diego Maradona (Napoli legacy), Zlatan Ibrahimović, Kaká, Andriy Shevchenko.
- **Iconic Managers:** Arrigo Sacchi, Carlo Ancelotti, Giovanni Trapattoni, Marcello Lippi, Fabio Capello, Massimiliano Allegri, Antonio Conte, Luciano Spalletti.
- **Records & Milestones:** Capocannoniere (top scorer record: Gonzalo Higuaín / Ciro Immobile with 36 goals in a season), Paolo Maldini / Gianluigi Buffon all-time appearance records (647+ matches), Scudetto badge history.
- **Historic Stadiums:** San Siro (Giuseppe Meazza), Stadio Olimpico (Rome), Stadio Diego Armando Maradona (San Paolo), Allianz Stadium (Turin), Stadio Luigi Ferraris.

## Ambiguity Traps & Exclusions
- Distinguish between AC Milan and Inter Milan home stadium naming convention: San Siro vs Stadio Giuseppe Meazza.
- Keep focus on Italian league accomplishments rather than general international tournaments.
