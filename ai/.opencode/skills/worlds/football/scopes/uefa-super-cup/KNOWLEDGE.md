# Scope Knowledge Profile: uefa-super-cup

## Identity / Boundaries
One fixture a year between two European champions. A fact belongs here only if it is about the Super Cup match itself.

## Experience-Native Knowledge
Fans remember the Super Cup as the opening trophy of a European season, and as the night two champions meet with little at stake but the honour.

## Strong Content Territories
Strong: Memorable finals, surprise winners from the second competition, famous goals, the fixture's change of format.

## Weak / Boring Territories
Weak: Attendance figures, minor substitutions, and anything that only restates a Champions League result.

## Evidence Opportunities
Dated match reports and official UEFA edition pages. Image and audio should come from the established media workflow.

## Terminology / Aliases
- UEFA Super Cup, European Super Cup, Super Coupe.
- Arabic: كأس السوبر الأوروبي، السوبر الأوروبي.
- The European Cup became the UEFA Champions League, and the UEFA Cup became the UEFA Europa League. Use the name that fits the edition.

## Ambiguity Traps
- Do not confuse this fixture with domestic super cups or the Club World Cup.
- The opponent's route changed over time: it was the Cup Winners' Cup winner before that competition ended, then the UEFA Cup winner.
- The fixture has been played over two legs and as a single match, so state the format when a question depends on it.
- Say whether an aggregate score or a single match is meant.

## Scope-Specific Taste
Questions must feel like a remembered European night, not a results table.

## Volatile Facts
Everything below is a stable fact about the fixture. Anything that changes with a new edition needs research before use, with a dated source: winners lists, title counts, records and the latest result. None of those are stated here on purpose.

# UEFA Super Cup Knowledge Base (قاعدة معرفة كأس السوبر الأوروبي)

## Scope Summary
A single annual fixture that opens the European season. Content must prioritize specific finals and what made them memorable.

## Key Territories
- **Origins:** The fixture began in the early 1970s as a meeting of European champions, and was later recognized by UEFA.
- **Format:** It was long played over two legs, then moved to a single match.
- **Venues:** Monaco hosted the single-match final for many years, and the fixture later moved between different cities.
- **Participants:** The champions of Europe meet the winners of Europe's second club competition.

## Ambiguity Traps & Exclusions
- A club's Super Cup win is not a Champions League win. Keep the two apart.
- Do not state a winners count, a record holder or a most recent winner without a dated source.
