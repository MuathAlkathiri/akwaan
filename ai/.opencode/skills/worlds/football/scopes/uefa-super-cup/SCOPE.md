- `scopeId`: `football.uefa-super-cup`
- `worldId`: `football`
- Boundary: The UEFA Super Cup and its earlier European Super Cup era, played between the champions of Europe's two leading club competitions.
- Included: Editions, finalists, winners, matches, goals, venues, and the format of the fixture across its history.
- Excluded: Champions League and Europa League campaigns, except to state how a club qualified; domestic super cups; club histories beyond this fixture.

- Pattern exclusions: live rankings, disputed unofficial records, and any edition named without its year.
- Safety & Precision: Anchor every fact to a named edition and year.
