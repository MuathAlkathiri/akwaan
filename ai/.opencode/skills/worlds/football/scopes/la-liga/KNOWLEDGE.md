# La Liga Knowledge Base (قاعدة معرفة الدوري الإسباني)

## Scope Summary
The premier men's football league in Spain. Content must prioritize league identity, historic rivalry moments, club traditions, stadiums, and individual player/manager records.

## Key Territories & Recognizable Entities
- **Iconic Clubs:** Real Madrid, FC Barcelona, Atlético Madrid, Athletic Club Bilbao, Valencia CF, Sevilla FC, Real Betis, Villarreal CF, Real Sociedad, Deportivo La Coruña, Celta Vigo, Espanyol.
- **Historic Derbies & Matches:** El Clásico (Real Madrid vs Barcelona), Derbi Madrileño (Real vs Atlético), El Gran Derbi (Sevilla vs Real Betis), Basque Derby (Athletic vs Real Sociedad).
- **Legendary Players & Eras:** Lionel Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo, Raúl González, Alfredo Di Stéfano, Telmo Zarra, Ronaldinho, Andrés Iniesta, Xavi Hernández, Hugo Sánchez, Iker Casillas, Fernando Hierro, Diego Simeone (as player/coach), Antoine Griezmann, Luka Modrić, Karim Benzema.
- **Prestigious Awards & Records:** Pichichi Trophy (top scorer), Zamora Trophy (best goalkeeper), Zarra Trophy (top Spanish scorer), 100-point league records (Mourinho 2011-12, Vilanova 2012-13).
- **Iconic Stadiums:** Santiago Bernabéu, Camp Nou (Spotify Camp Nou), Cívitas Metropolitano / Vicente Calderón, San Mamés, Mestalla, Ramón Sánchez-Pizjuán, Benito Villamarín, Reale Arena (Anoeta).

## Ambiguity Traps & Exclusions
- Do not confuse UEFA Champions League accomplishments with domestic La Liga accomplishments (e.g. European titles belong to European competition).
- Anchor live records (e.g. all-time Pichichi records: Messi 474 goals, Cristiano Ronaldo 311 goals, Telmo Zarra 251 goals).
