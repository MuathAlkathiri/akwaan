# Saudi National Team Knowledge Base (قاعدة معرفة المنتخب السعودي)

## Scope Summary
The men's senior team of Saudi Arabia, a member of the Asian Football Confederation. Content must prioritize tournament history and moments the country remembers.

## Key Territories
- **World Cup:** The team made its World Cup debut in 1994 and reached the round of 16 that year. It beat Argentina in the group stage at the 2022 World Cup.
- **Asian Cup:** The team won the AFC Asian Cup in 1984, 1988 and 1996.
- **Regional Football:** The Arabian Gulf Cup is a long-running part of the team's identity.
- **Iconic Moments:** Saeed Al-Owairan's solo goal against Belgium at the 1994 World Cup and Salem Al-Dawsari's winner against Argentina in 2022.

## Ambiguity Traps & Exclusions
- A World Cup question about this team belongs here. One about the tournament itself belongs to the World Cup Scope.
- Do not name a current coach, captain or squad member as current without a dated source.
