# Football Legends Knowledge Base (قاعدة معرفة أساطير كرة القدم)

## Scope Summary
A global celebration of the greatest figures in football history across eras, emphasizing universally recognized legends, career milestones, and iconic historical feats.

## Key Territories & Recognizable Entities
- **The Pantheon of Legends:** Pelé (O Rei / 3 World Cups), Diego Maradona (Hand of God / 1986 hero), Johan Cruyff (Total Football / Ajax / Barcelona), Zinedine Zidane (1998 World Cup / 2002 volley), Ronaldo Nazário (El Fenomeno / 2002 World Cup), Ronaldinho (Joga Bonito / Ballon d'Or 2005), Franz Beckenbauer (Der Kaiser / captain & manager World Cup winner), Lev Yashin (The Black Spider / only GK Ballon d'Or), Ferenc Puskás (Mighty Magyars / Real Madrid / Puskás Award), Eusébio (Black Panther / Benfica / 1966), Michel Platini (3 consecutive Ballons d'Or 1983-85), Marco van Basten, George Best, Bobby Charlton, Garrincha, Zico, Romário, Roberto Baggio, Thierry Henry.
- **Legendary Managers:** Sir Alex Ferguson (Manchester United 26-year dynasty / 13 Premier Leagues), Rinus Michels (Father of Total Football), Johan Cruyff (Dream Team), Arrigo Sacchi (Milan pressing revolution), Bill Shankly & Bob Paisley (Liverpool dynasty), Brian Clough (Nottingham Forest back-to-back European Cups), Helenio Herrera (Catenaccio mastermind), Pep Guardiola, Carlo Ancelotti, José Mourinho.
- **Historic Accolades & Milestones:** Ballon d'Or records, Golden Foot, FIFA World Cup Golden Balls, 1000+ career goals milestones (Pelé, Romário, Bican), iconic shirt numbers (#10, #7, #9, #14).

## Ambiguity Traps & Exclusions
- Ensure questions test recognizable legend milestones rather than minute statistics.
- Avoid obscure journeymen; maintain an undeniable "Legend" threshold.
