# Experience-First Contract

## Canonical Authoring Principle

> **"Never begin by asking what trivia facts can I write.**
> **Begin by asking what interaction/question shape would be fun to play.**
> **Then find a recognizable, fair fact that fits that shape."**

## Canonical 7-Step Generation Order

```text
1. GLOBAL QUESTION CRAFT (.opencode/knowledge/architecture/QUESTION-CRAFT.md)
   ↓ (12 pillars of question craft, anti-pattern gates, natural Arabic copy)
2. QUESTION ARCHETYPE SELECTION (.opencode/knowledge/architecture/QUESTION-ARCHETYPES.md)
   ↓ (Select from 20 canonical play shapes: Name Fragment, Reverse, Connection, etc.)
3. WORLD GUIDANCE (.opencode/skills/worlds/<world>/WORLD.md)
   ↓ (Consult World Question Palette and rotate content dimensions)
4. SCOPE & KNOWLEDGE GUIDANCE (.opencode/skills/worlds/<world>/scopes/<scope>/KNOWLEDGE.md)
   ↓ (Ground in authentic, durable canonical facts)
5. MECHANIC COMPATIBILITY (.opencode/knowledge/architecture/MECHANIC-COMPATIBILITY.md)
   ↓ (Enforce mechanic payload contracts: Bomb, Combo, Marhala, RYO, Closest, etc.)
6. BATCH VARIETY REVIEW (.opencode/knowledge/architecture/BATCH-VARIETY.md)
   ↓ (Audit set-level archetype concentration <= 35%, entity rotation, opening phrasing)
7. FACT, ANSWER & ZERO LEAKAGE QA (.agents/skills/akwaan-content-qa/SKILL.md)
   ↓ (Validate deterministic truth, normalized accepted answers, zero prompt leaks)
CONTENT ITEM READY FOR HUMAN PRODUCT REVIEW
```

An item fails when players merely retrieve a fact without producing the ChallengeType's intended social moment. Factual accuracy is necessary but never sufficient.
